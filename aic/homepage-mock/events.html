Events · Latin Forum

A named room inside a host.

Spanish-first. Bolted onto someone else’s conference. Not a two-day owned flagship. AIC does not need their full production.

What it is

Latino Corner / Latin Forum. Same product.

LatAm executives meeting Bay Area AI operators. A partner room, a session slot, or a side-event tent. Staff ceiling: two or three people. Volunteer table hosts allowed.

A reverse-pitch flagship, when AIC runs one of its own, is not this page. This page is the room inside a host.

The offer — top three

Ranked for a bolt-on.

1

Default

Mesa redonda

Facilitated executive roundtable. Todos participan. Sin escenario, sin slides, un solo prompt.

Time 75–90 min, daytime Size 8–15 seated peers Language Spanish-first Staff 1 facilitator + 1 closer

One table. Everyone a participant. Five-minute frame, then one prompt. Last ten minutes: written next step, then the closer books 20-minute 1:1s. Founders are not at the mesa unless invited as one or two operators.

2

Session slot

Reverse-pitch / buyer briefing

Compradores al micrófono. El ejecutivo brief del problema; los founders escuchan.

Time 60–75 min + 1:1s Size 4–6 execs · room as given Language Execs brief in Spanish When The host gives a session slot

Executives give a short problem brief — not a company commercial — then double-opt-in 1:1s in the same room. Use when the host already runs customer-voice sessions.

3

Side grid

Curated meetup + 1:1

El grupo primero; la conversación privada después.

Time 45–60 min + 1:1s Size 12–25, pre-clustered Language Spanish-first When The host only gives a side-event grid

Clustered by role or problem — not an open mixer. Frame, structured mixing, write who you need, then a small 1:1 block.

Evening close

Not a second product

La Cena

Same guest list as the daytime Forum. Informal table. One conversation. The Forum converts; the cena makes it a relationship.

Time 90–120 min, evening Size 8–12, one restaurant table Language Spanish-first Staff Host + closer

Researched · not first touch

Four to six stay collapsed.
