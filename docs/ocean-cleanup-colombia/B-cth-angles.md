# Coffee brief: CleantechHUB angles for The Ocean Cleanup in Colombia

Research only. Nothing here is an offer, an email, or a commitment. Written 16 October 2026 for Gideon Blaauw (CleantechHUB, Bogotá).

## Where they actually are

The Ocean Cleanup (Rotterdam) is in Colombia as country 13 of its **30 Cities Program**, on signed memoranda, not on deployed Interceptors. Santa Marta (19 August 2026) covers the Manzanares and the Gaira before they reach the Caribbean; CORPAMAG handles permits and has a Green Business Program lined up for the catch. Barranquilla (21 September 2026) points at the lower Magdalena and Arroyo León, where the city already runs a trash trap, and at the Ciénaga de Mallorquín. The public voice is Ricardo Restrepo, Regional Director for the Americas.

Their own model names the seats they do not fill: a local **operator** who pulls the waste out, a **waste handler** who sorts and recycles what can be recycled, a **city** that permits the work and ideally pays a collection fee, a **sponsor** for deployment and operations, and someone local who already knows the institutions. River plastic is not the Kia trunk-liner story. That product is ocean plastic from the Great Pacific Garbage Patch, under a chain-of-custody standard. River catch stays in the local waste system. UNDP is named as a global deployment partner. They also gather their own river data (drones, GPS drifters, cameras, trash composition) and the Interceptor texts the operator when the dumpsters are full.

Do not offer to run an Interceptor. The open seats are downstream, data, money, and introductions.

What CTH can actually put on the table: national lead for ClimateLaunchpad Colombia (EIT Climate-KIC) since 2019 and the Nexus portfolio; Datalab with Externado and Air4U; completed UNDP-AFCIA rounds; an AuRA / BID Lab support-organization contract; one Dutch Embassy conversation on cooperatives; and the energy-coop desk.

## Six angles

**1. The Green Business seat in Santa Marta, with one named startup.** CORPAMAG already said the memorandum should look for sustainable uses of the collected waste. Plásticos Ambientales is the whole CLAC 2024 cohort (plastic waste and construction) and already works with Corseplast, a 2024 recycler cooperative for flexible plastics. That is a handler conversation, not a technology pitch.

Why: their published gap is a local business model for river waste, and CTH already accelerated one company in that gap.

First step: ask if the Green Business list has names. If it is empty, send a five-name Nexus shortlist within a week, Plásticos Ambientales first, marked confidential.

**2. Use Xiclo’s Santa Marta non-launch as the lesson.** Xiclo, in the Nexus portfolio, was the Colombian operator of the GIZ / ANDI ProUsar reusable-packaging pilot (October 2023–March 2024). The pilot report says Santa Marta was in scope, brands were interested, and the team then chose not to open there: logistics, washing, and software could not be staffed, and the calendar and local reception got in the way. Bogotá went first.

Why: 30 Cities is about stopping inflow. A reuse pilot that already stopped on this coast is more useful than a new idea.

First step: ask whether source reduction sits inside the memorandum or only interception. Reopen Xiclo only if they want that seat, and only to hear what blocked Santa Marta.

**3. A monitoring sprint in the Datalab, not a sensor sale.** CTH’s analogue is the Living Lab on datalab.cleantechhub.net with Air4U (air-quality CSV files, not river sensors) under the Externado convenio, plus a closed Datathon room. The nearest public window is Externado entrepreneurship week in early November. The Bucaramanga energy-cluster sprint does not have a locked date.

Why: both memoranda already include research and monitoring, and CTH does not have river hardware.

First step: offer one waste-composition dashboard on a single river, with The Ocean Cleanup as challenge owner and Datalab as the room. Do not offer Air4U sensors for a river.

**4. UNDP for the funder seat. BID Lab only as a startup slot.** CTH completed UNDP-AFCIA rounds 1 and 2 (adaptation incubation and acceleration, 2021–2025). The Ocean Cleanup already lists UNDP as a deployment partner. On BID Lab, be precise: CTH has not executed a BID Lab project of its own. It is contracted as an ecosystem support organization on AuRA (Fundación Bolívar Davivienda and BID Lab, convenio CNV-04285, through 31 January 2029), with a 2026 slot of three Colombian companies.

Why: both memoranda are still pre-deployment, so the open money is a sponsor, and UNDP is the one name both sides already use.

First step: ask which line is actually open (interceptor hardware, operator running costs, or monitoring) before naming a donor. Mention AuRA only if a Colombian waste startup needs a programme slot.

**5. One Dutch introduction, and only if the seat is empty.** You already sat with the Embassy’s economic section in August 2026 on energy cooperatives (Albert Kobus; no written minutes). CTH also delivered the Netherlands-embassy-backed CCE Hub in El Salvador (2024–2025). That is a credential, not a partnership on this project.

Why: they say deployment is easier with someone who already has the local doors.

First step: ask who in Rotterdam and at the Bogotá embassy is already on the Colombia file. Offer a single intro only if that name is missing. Do not promise an embassy meeting.

**6. Operator design from the energy-coop work, not an energy pitch.** The Energy Coop desk (with PASO Colombia; platform at energiacoop.cleantechhub.net) is about who owns a local operation: a member association, a city fee, a blended stack. The Ocean Cleanup says the same thing in other words: an operator for the daily pull-out, and collection fees from the city so the work does not stop when the sponsor does. Dutch cooperative statutes are an internal reference only. Do not claim an Energie Samen or DGRV endorsement, and do not describe PASO as an energy operator.

Why: the missing piece on both coasts is who runs the cleanup every morning.

First step: ask whether they want a company, a utility, or a community association. Map the coop pattern only if they say community.

## Questions to ask

1. Of the two memoranda, which one has a named operator and a named waste handler, and which is still choosing the site?
2. Barranquilla already traps trash on Arroyo León. Are you replacing that trap, measuring it, or looking at a different point on the Magdalena?
3. Does CORPAMAG’s Green Business Program have companies on it, or is that list empty?
4. Which budget line is open, and is UNDP already in the Colombia conversation?
5. Who at the embassy or in Rotterdam should CleantechHUB not introduce twice?
