Documentary handheld footage on a mountain pull-off in the Colombian Andes. A woman truck driver holding the door handle of her dusty truck looks down the winding mountain road, then climbs up into the cab. Low clouds drift slowly over green ridges; far below, a cargo truck crawls along the switchback road. Camera slowly eases back to reveal the corridor. Real skin texture, sweat, dust, no makeup. Muted gritty color, 35mm documentary look, subtle handheld camera. No dialogue, mouth closed.
