Frequently Asked Questions

How can I pitch Collide Capital and who are you looking to invest in?

Pitch us here. A member of the Investment team reviews all inbound pitches and we will reach out if we believe you a good fit within our thesis.

What is Collide Capital’s investment thesis?

Collide Capital invests in early-stage companies from pre-seed to Series A, with a focus on founders building in fintech, supply chain, and future of work. We believe the next generation of category-defining companies will be forged through collision – of culture and code, of policy and product, and of perspectives too often overlooked by capital. And we believe in founders who are relentlessly tactical, deeply connected to the problem they’re solving and unafraid to build in the face of doubt.

Is Collide Capital accepting new investors?

Not at this time. Please reach out to investors@collidecap.com to learn about future investment opportunities and subscribe to our newsletter to stay informed.

Who are Aaron & Brian?

Collide Capital founders Aaron Samuels and Brian Hollins built two of the largest Black tech ecosystems, AfroTech and BLCK VC, to connect under-networked and overlooked communities to institutional resources. With an oversubscribed Fund II and over $170M AUM, they seek to invest in the most innovative and hard-working founders solving the next generation of global challenges. At their core, Aaron and Brian understand the power that community has to create opportunity. Learn more by reading the Letter From Our Founders.

Follow us on LinkedIn, Instagram and Luma, and subscribe to our newsletter to hear what we're up to at our firm and within our portfolio.
