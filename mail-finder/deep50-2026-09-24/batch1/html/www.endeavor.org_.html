We are the Global Network of Trust of, by, and for entrepreneurs.

Why We Do It: To build thriving entrepreneurial ecosystems in elsewhere markets around the world.

Moniepoint

 Tosin Eniolorunda & Felix Ike Fintech Africa

Carsome

 Eric Cheng Marketplaces Asia

Carsome offers a fast, fair, and free car selling service with full transparency through a one-stop online auction platform. In 2021, Carsome became Malaysia’s first technology unicorn.

Wellhub

Instadeep

 Karim Beguir B2B Software Africa

InstaDeep is an AI company building predictive analytics solutions for enterprise clients, particularly in the biomedical, mobility and transportation spaces.

Kitopi

Kitopi is a managed cloud kitchen platform. It allows restaurants to open delivery-only locations by providing the necessary infrastructure and software with minimal capital expenditures and time as well as taking care of the entire customer experience journey.

Tyme

 Tjaart van der Walt & Coen Jonker Fintech Africa

Tyme is a multi-country digital banking group, focused on bridging the digital divide in emerging markets, by integrating into physical retail ecosystems.
