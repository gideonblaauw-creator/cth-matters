Regenerative transition
as an investment axis

  • Back to home
  • Next

At Regenera Ventures Fund, regenerative transition means shifting from industrialized agricultural models toward systems that:

  • Restore ecosystems
  • Strengthen rural communities
  • Ensure long-term economic viability

We identify four key transitions driving this transformation in Mexico’s agricultural and livestock systems:

Bridge-building between
capital and the Regenerative
Transition.

As a pioneer fund, we invest holistically from farm to fork across the regenerative
value-web in mexico — bridging smallholder farmers, market innovators and regenerative
enablers to create economic prosperity that is aligned with climate resilience for
people and the planet.

Capital at the
service of life.

Regenerating productive ecosystems restores health &
biodiversity — strengthening both nature, social justice and
the economy.

We don’t impose regeneration; we unleash it’s potential,
partnering with local regenerators who bring life back to
the land and water ecosystems.

Learn how we see transitioning from a
vicious cycle to a virtuous one

  • Healthy soils
  • Greater biodiversity
  • Economic resilence in
    communities with social justice

Financing the
regenerative
transition.

As an impact-first fund, we provide flexible,
aligned long-term capital and tailored technical
support to communities, cooperatives, and
companies that have transitioned, are helping to
transition, or aim to transition to regenerative
management by diversifying their incomes, building
climate resilience, and improving profitability.

We invest holistically
from farm to fork.

Investment profiles

Regenerative Enablers

Solutions for the regenerative transition: Biological inputs, soil & water management, monetization of ecosystem services (such as carbon, biodiversity, water bioenergy and biomass) and inclusive rural finance.

Investment profiles

Market Game-Changers

Conscious retail and brands (food, biocosmetics),
direct to consumer digital platforms, and
regenerative ecotourism.

Investment profiles

Direct Product Engagement

Regenerative agriculture, livestock,
aquaculture, fishering and forestry.

Investment profiles

Value Web Integration

Regenerative food processing, sustainable logistics,
fair trade, and value-added cooperatives.
