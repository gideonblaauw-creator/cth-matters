Partnership Between Seedz and Coopercitrus: Digital Solutions Meet Rural Presence

Seedz, an agtech specializing in digital solutions for agribusiness, has formed a strategic partnership with Coopercitrus, one of Brazil’s largest agricultural cooperatives. The goal is to calibrate and enhance Seedz’s crop monitoring technology, which uses satellites and artificial intelligence, with real data collected in the field. Over 500 Coopercitrus employees contribute to refining the algorithms, helping reduce noise and increase accuracy in monitoring various crops.

Through this collaboration, Seedz expands its operational capacity beyond soy and corn, gaining access to Coopercitrus’ scale and diversity, which includes over 42,000 members and revenues exceeding R$ 8 billion. For the startup, the alliance strengthens its position as a key player in digital agribusiness, integrating technology, loyalty, and geospatial intelligence into solutions that bring rural producers closer to the digital world.
