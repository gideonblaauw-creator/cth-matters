OUR STORY

At Actyus, we don’t just invest in companies — we invest in the future. We are an alternative investment management and venture capital firm committed to innovation and sustainable growth.

Our approach goes beyond traditional investments: we are pioneers in proprietary management strategies such as Venture Debt, Fintech, and Private Equity, and we also manage real estate and secondary assets with the same passion and forward-looking vision. At Actyus, we build opportunities, drive change, and strive to foster the creation of a better world.
