> About Us

Arxus Web Group was founded to help small and mid-size companies integrate the digital world into their sales and marketing cycle. Our mission is to help our clients increase their sales, get new customers, and make existing customer relationships stronger. We call our company a digital marketing agency.

Arxus was created by Greg Paulhus, a former strategist and consultant with one of Canada's top ten web design and development firms.

Greg has years of experience producing high end Web projects for many different kinds of clients. It was out of this experience that the concept of Arxus was formed. Greg saw a need in the business community for a company that focused on helping businesses use the Web as a powerful marketing tool.

Arxus Web Group is not simply a website design and development company. We're also not an advertising agency or a communications consulting firm. We're something different. We're something new. Arxus is your digital marketing agency.
