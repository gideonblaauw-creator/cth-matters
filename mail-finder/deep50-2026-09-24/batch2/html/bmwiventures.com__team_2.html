Marcus Behrendt

Managing Partner

Marcus has been a Managing Partner at BMW i Ventures since joining in 2018.

Previously, he spent 5 years with BMW as VP User Experience (Control Panels, Ergonomics) and as a Senior Manager and then VP in HMI Concepts and Concept Quality. Prior to BMW he was Head of European Operations at JD Power for 7 years, spent 7 years with Mercedes-Benz in various project management roles, and was a Business Development Specialist at Herlitz PBS for 3 years. For many years, Marcus was an active volunteer and later Chairman for the Verein Berliner Austauschschüler and Stiftung Austausch Berlin, which are both foundations supporting youth exchange.

Marcus holds a degree in Management and Engineering from Technische Universität Berlin and an Executive Degree in Venture Capital from UC Berkeley - Haas School of Business.

Kasper Sage

Managing Partner

Kasper has been with BMW i Ventures since 2016 and serves as a Managing Partner. He has spent most of his career as a venture capital investor in Silicon Valley.  

Previously he was part of 7Innovate, the venture capital arm of ProsiebenSat1 Media SE, focused on early stage investments in Silicon Valley, Israel, and Asia. Baris Guzel

Partner

Baris joined BMW i Ventures in 2017 and is a Partner.

Previously, he worked with several VC funds: Presidio Partners, The Halo Funds, and New Commercial Room, and top-tier investment banks on Wall Street: BofA Merrill Lynch and Deutsche Bank. During his time at Deutsche Bank, Baris advised technology companies on strategic decisions, M&A, and IPO transactions.

Michael Hammer

CFO

Michael has been with BMW i Ventures since 2016 building the general fund structure, investment process and operations from scratch as CFO. He oversees all operational and financial matters on a daily basis, and interacts as the interface to the LP and the fund operations in Luxembourg.

Previously, he spent 5 years with BMW Group as Investment Manager for BMW’s pension assets and strategic liquidity with assets under management of more than $20bn. In addition, Michael managed the BMW foundation’s capital implementing one of the first impact investing strategies in Europe. Prior to BMW he was a Senior Investment Consultant for 5 years at Mercer Oliver Wyman, advising large institutional investors on asset allocation, alternative investment strategies, asset manager selection and implementing derivative hedging portfolios.

Michael holds an MA (Diploma) in Financial Mathematics and Economics from the University of Ulm.

Inga Grieger

Business Development Manager

Inga G. Grieger is Business Development Manager and Managing Director at BMW i Ventures. Samantha Huang

Principal

Samantha Huang is a Principal at BMW i Ventures, where she focuses on investments across AI and big data, automotive, Industry 4.0, sustainability, and the next generation of human experiences. She also serves as Head of Content and Board Director for the Emerging Venture Capitalists Association, a nonprofit organization for empowering the next generation of venture capitalists.

Scott Walbrun

Principal

Scott Walbrun is a Principal at BMW i Ventures, where he focuses on Series A & B investments across clean tech, mobility, software, and robotics. In this capacity, Scott serves on the board as a director or observer for seven companies, and he was recognized in 2022 as a Global Corporate Venturing Rising Star. Outside of BMW i Ventures, he serves as a mentor and workshop leader for hundreds of startups through various accelerator programs across the country, including at Argonne National Labs.

Prior to joining BMW i Ventures, Scott provided M&A and capital markets advisory as a member of Jefferies’ Technology Investment Banking team. Scott joined Jefferies after completing his MBA at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business. While completing his MBA, he worked at Kinzie Capital Partners evaluating private equity investments, as well as at the Polsky Center for Entrepreneurship and Innovation supporting the commercialization of science-based and deep tech companies.

Prior to business school, Scott managed Strategic Finance and FP&A at Truck Hero (a TA Associates portfolio company) and began his career in Fiat Chrysler’s Finance Leadership Development Program.
