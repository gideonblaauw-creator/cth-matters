About Us

Becky Berk,  M.Ed., is the founder and principal consultant of Cross-Boundary Consulting.  
She has worked with schools and non-profits in New Hampshire for over twenty years.   



Becky’s eclectic interests have evolved over twenty five years from animal behavior, (she tracked grizzly bears in Alaska as an undergraduate), to the way organizations support and shape the behavior of their members.  Becky is the founder of Cross-Boundary Consulting, dedicated to helping organizations communicate and collaborate more effectively with internal and external constituencies. 

 

With a degree from Swarthmore College, Becky taught biology and physics at the middle and high school level at the Derryfield School in Manchester, New Hampshire and at Cairo American College in Cairo, Egypt, while also directing community service programs for students.  She has twice been elected to her local School Board, and her work was widely credited with the passage of a $21 million construction and renovation bond for two schools in town.  She received a Master's in Education for Administration, Supervision and Leadership from Antioch University New England, focusing on the way organizations create or stifle a sense of "vested" community through their management of internal and external constituencies.  


More recently, Becky has served as the Associate Director of the New Hampshire Center for Effective Behavioral Interventions and Supports, focused on helping schools to reduce problem behavior and promote positive school culture through effective interpersonal and organizational practices. She co-authored an article called "Responsiveness to Family Engagement Practices" which appeared in Teaching Exceptional Children, and she has presented several trainings and seminars on building effective cross-functional teams. 

 

Second only to her passion for education, Becky is committed to social justice and the preservation of dignity for all people.  Her non-profit background in New Hampshire includes leadership positions with United Way, where she directed community planning and fund distribution activities, and with the American Lung Association (now Breathe NH) where she directed the statewide government relations and advocacy program.  Becky has also served on the Board of Directors of several non-profits, including NH Community Loan Fund, Child and Family Services and Webster House.

 

Becky lives with her husband, two teenagers and a small menagerie in Hooksett, New Hampshire.
