J.P. Keating – Startup Investor Profile

General Info

J.P. Keating, Managing Partner & Founder of proChain Ventures, focuses on AI-enabled supply chain and mobility technologies for societal good. He invests in Pre-Seed and Seed stage startups in supply chain SaaS, deep tech, aerospace, applied AI, and defense. With over 25 years of experience, his track record includes investments in companies like Intramotev, Finlogistics, Natilus, and more. J.P. Keating's investment check size ranges from $10K to $250K for Seed VC investments. He is known for his hands-on approach, actively partnering with generalist funds and supporting operational transformation in supply chains. He values partnerships and collaboration, aiming to bring value to founders and accelerate their success.
