Let’s Build Together the Future of Elections and Democracy

ELECTIS is a non-governmental organization based on the following principles: non profit, non commercial, and politically and philosophically neutral.

Our core purpose is to help bettering our democratic systems by promoting the best usage of technologies ("Tech for Democracy"), with a specific focus on electoral processes which are at the core of democratic life. We aim to achieve it through three main tools:

  • Create a large cross-border community of engaged and committed citizens, able to debate, assess and promote democratically concrete solutions and usages;

  • Catalyze concrete projects and test cases connecting communities and institutions with existing solutions, ensuring traction and visibility to feed the general debate;

  • Spur R&D on new or enhanced solutions, based on feedbacks and debates within the community around test cases and developments globally.

The Community: The Elecits Community pulls together individuals and organizations (public and NGOs) throughout the world that share a common commitment to enhance democracy through better usage of technology. It rallies ambassadors and doers to debate, assess and promote existing and future tools, creating and spreading best practices.The Community is politically, economically and philosophically neutral. It is organized in a horizontal and democratic way.

The Projects: Electis acts as a catalyst for the organization of test cases. It helps putting together a voting community (whether already institutionalized or not) and new voting solutions. These elections are powered by the Community, to give them maximum traction and visibility, and then debated at large with all committed citizens to infuse lessons learned into the general discussion.

The Solutions: Electis acts an observatory of new electoral solutions throughout the world. These solutions are debated within the Community and possibly used in Electis powered Elections. Electis itself does not aim at becoming a solution provider, but is nevertheless working on developing its own solutions to help feed the R&D and ensure the availability of free-of-use solutions.
