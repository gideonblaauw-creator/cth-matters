Open Bosch

Who we are

Open Bosch is the Bosch group's venture client unit. We test leading startup solutions and make it easy to partner with Bosch.

If you want to collaborate with us, please get in touch.

What we do

We support collaboration between Startups and business units from Bosch. Our systematic and scalable process ensures maximum business impact for both parties.

An open innovation culture is the key to faster solutions in an increasingly complex world.

Bosch Chairman, Dr. Stefan Hartung

How we work

Open Bosch applies the so-called Venture Client process. We support business units to identify the potential for external partners from the startup world, help them finding the right company and entering first collaboration projects (pilot). The pilot serves to quickly validate the technology and cultural fit for a given Bosch use case, and to build a successful partnership or supplier relationship.

What's in it for startups?

Open Bosch offers startups that match a Bosch use case to find new customers or partners at Bosch quickly. First pilots are financially compensated by Bosch.

Sell & Scale

We pay you for a first pilot project and connect you with the right stakeholders at Bosch, also to scale your solution internally!

Test & Learn

Integrate your solution in a Bosch use case, collect data and improve quickly. Gain new technical and market knowledge.

Accelerate

We ensure an efficient pilot purchasing process for a fast and lean collaboration.

What's in it for Bosch business units?

To make it as convenient as possible for Bosch teams to partner up with a startup, Open Bosch supports from end to end.

Co-Innovate

Become a partner of innovative startups. We support you on the whole process from identifying partnering opportunities to establishing sustainable partnerships.

Accelerate

Accelerate startup collaboration through a standardized pilot process. From initial contact to start of pilot within 3 weeks including purchasing.

Open Bosch Award 2025

Bosch honors impactful startup partnerships

Tanja Rückert recognizes the outstanding startup partnership between Bosch and Dotphoton with the Open Bosch Award. Its AI-ready data compression helps build safer and smarter automotive systems.

The 7th Open Bosch Award, held on November 7, 2025, honored the most outstanding startup partnerships across the Bosch Group. This year’s process saw 70 applications from more than 20 business units, with seven teams reaching the finals. Each partnership demonstrated how startup partnerships drive measurable results for Bosch customers and business units alike.

During the ceremony, Tanja Rückert, member of the board of management and chief digital officer, highlighted how startup partnerships strengthen Bosch from within: “It is not only about beneficial collaborations with partners but also within our company. That makes us much faster and stronger.”

Philipp Rose, managing director of RBVC, emphasized the strategic importance of these collaborations:“With Open Bosch, we invest in the support of promising partnerships, not just technology. Business impact always comes first.

Winner: Dotphoton and Cross-Domain Computing Solutions (XC)

AI-ready data compression for safer and smarter automotive systems

The winning partnership between Dotphoton and Bosch Cross-Domain Computing Solutions (XC) impressed with its tangible results in advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS). The collaboration began in 2020, when Bosch and Dotphoton started working on AI-ready image compression for safety-critical automotive applications.

The solution reduces data storage costs by up to 80 percent while ensuring the reliability of critical functions such as emergency braking. It also enables faster data transfer and helps reduce CO₂ emissions associated with data storage and processing. Following successful implementation, Bosch is now transferring Dotphoton’s technology more broadly into the automotive sector, opening further opportunities for collaboration and scalability.

Finalists 2025: Startup collaborations creating real business impact

NewSensorIoT × Cross-Domain Computing Solutions China (XC-CN)

Accelerating ADAS HMI development in record time

XC China collaborated with NewSensorIoT (Hangzhou Xinzhi IOT Technology Co., Ltd. 杭州新知物联科技有限公司) to integrate a customer (OEM) demand 3D environment rendering solution into Bosch’s Mid-Trim automated driving and parking assistance systems. The result is a faithful photorealistic environment rendering and accurate perception capabilities, to build driver trust in advanced driver assistance systems and perceived safety, strengthening Bosch’s position in China’s competitive automotive market. The project was realized from first contact to production in less than a year, an example of startup agility meeting Bosch scale.

SPARETECH × Bosch Mobility

AI-driven spare parts management for manufacturing efficiency

Through its 6+ years partnership with SPARETECH, Bosch has introduced an AI-powered spare parts management and maintenance platform that improves master data quality, identifies duplicates to minimize inventory & procurement costs and reduces unplanned machine downtimes. The solution is already deployed at more than 22+ Bosch Mobility plants and also deployed in Bosch entities outside Bosch Mobility. The solution is being rolled out globally, helping manufacturing sites operate more efficiently and sustainably.

BLUESKEYE AI × Bosch Global Software Technologies (BGSW)

Emotion-aware personal car companion

Bosch partnered with BLUESKEYE AI to enhance Evoco, an intelligent in-car companion that recognizes emotions, adapts to driver behavior, and supports safe and engaging journeys. By combining emotion AI with Bosch’s automotive expertise, the system sets new standards for personalized in-cabin experiences and driver attention monitoring, meeting global automotive requirements.

ONEKEY × ETAS

Cybersecurity and software transparency for connected vehicles

Bosch is using the ONEKEY’s automated vulnerability management technology to optimise own product cybersecurity, while ETAS has integrated the ONEKEY platform into its CycurX security portfolio, helping ensure software compliance and protection for the next generation of connected vehicles. The collaboration strengthens Bosch’s cybersecurity offering and supports multiple business units developing software-defined vehicle platforms.

Katrick Technologies × Bosch Rodez and Bosch UK & Ireland

Industrializing green energy with wind panel technology

Bosch teams in the UK & Ireland and Bosch Rodez plant in France joined forces with Katrick Technologies to industrialize patented Wind Panels that capture ground-level wind energy. The collaboration combines Bosch’s manufacturing expertise with a breakthrough clean-energy technology with prototypes to be installed at Scotland’s largest airport, opening new business opportunities in sustainable infrastructure.

The 2025 finalists illustrate how startup collaborations at Bosch combine speed, technology, and scale to deliver measurable results – from new business models and digital services to efficiency gains and sustainability impact.

FAQ

What is a Venture client unit?

Open Bosch is the Venture Client unit of the Bosch group. This means Open Bosch becomes an early client of the startup during the pilot phase. As a Venture Client we do not invest, meaning we do not ask for shares or equity.

Through this relationship, startups get a supplier status, supplier number, purchase order, and revenue.
Do you invest in startups?

No, but Robert Bosch Venture Capital does. Startups we evaluate for a partnership may also be introduced to Robert Bosch Venture Capital for an investment.
Do startups receive payment or funding?

Open Bosch becomes an early client, not your shareholder. You will receive payment for the pilot. How much we pay depends on your effort of integrating and validating your solution, including materials, travel, working hours, etc.

Is Open Bosch an accelerator?

No, we are a Venture Client for startups who have a proven track record and significant funding.
What happens after the Open Bosch program?

After the successful validation of your solution, Bosch aims to become a long-term partner or client of your startup.

You will negotiate further development and supplier agreements directly with the respective business unit and our purchasing department. These negotiations will ideally start during the pilot project.
How to get in contact?

If you would like to propose a partnership and get in touch with a Bosch department, please get in touch through Innovate-bosch
