Pauline de Valk

Investment Director ET

Contact information

LinkedIn

Pauline de Valk is Investment Director ET at the ABN AMRO Sustainable Impact Fund, focused on private equity and growth investments in the energy transition and clean mobility sectors. In this role, she combines technical knowledge with commercial strategy expertise to identify high-potential opportunities and deliver measurable value—both in financial returns and sustainable impact. Pauline currently serves on the board of ETPA and contributes to portfolio management at Urban Mine.

She began her career as a strategy consultant at Spring Associates, advising companies on growth strategies and supporting private equity firms in embedding sustainability into their investment practices.

Pauline holds a BSc in Industrial Design and an MSc in Mechanical Engineering from Delft University of Technology. She joined the Sustainable Impact Fund motivated by the conviction that integrating sustainability not only mitigates risks but also unlocks long-term value creation, positioning businesses at the forefront of future-proof ecosystems.
