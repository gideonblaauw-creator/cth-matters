About Manutara Ventures

Welcome to Manutara Ventures!

Manutara is a venture capital firm founded by experienced entrepreneurs. Our mission is to empower the next generation of entrepreneurs through strategic investments and hands-on support. Our Approach: Early and Hands-On We specialize in early-stage investments, typically at the pre-seed or seed stage, but can also do Series A. Our extensive experience in scaling businesses globally allows us to offer invaluable support right from the start.

We believe talent knows no borders

Entrepreneurs We Back

At Manutara, we are passionate about backing founders who embody the spirit of entrepreneurship. We seek out individuals who are not only ambitious but also possess a relentless drive to compete on a global stage. Ethics and integrity are paramount to us, as we believe that sustainable success is built on a foundation of trust and responsibility. Moreover, we value founders who thrive in a collaborative environment, recognizing that true innovation often emerges from diverse, cohesive teams. If you're a founder with big dreams, a commitment to excellence, and a willingness to work collaboratively, we're excited to learn more about your vision and how we can help you turn it into reality.
