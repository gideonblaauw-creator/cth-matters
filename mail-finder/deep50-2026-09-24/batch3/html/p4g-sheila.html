Sheila Carina

Subscribe to our newsletter
Regional Investment Manager, Asia

Sheila is the P4G Regional Investment Manager for Asia for the Partnering for Green Growth and the Global Goals 2030 (P4G) platform. She plays the key role in sourcing potential partnerships in Indonesia and Vietnam, managing them and accelerating their investment-readiness.  

Prior to joining P4G, Sheila was the technical coordinator for impact fund in ASSIST Project, UNDP Indonesia, whose mission is to support the development of impact investment landscape in Indonesia through technical assistance for venture capital and also start-ups in Indonesia. Her role includes creating impact thesis and impact due diligence for the venture capital as well as preparing thematic accelerator programs related to high priority agenda of Indonesia.

Sheila holds a Master of Science degree in Sustainability (Business, Environment and Corporate Responsibility) from University of Leeds. She holds a Bachelor’s degree in International Business Management from University of Nottingham Malaysia Campus. 

Sheila lives in Jakarta. She speaks Indonesian and English, and enjoys her free time going for karaoke and doing any sports.
