01The Practice

A selective family office — conviction over volume.

BEV manages the principal capital of Benjamin Radomski. It was formalised in 2023 to run a focused investing practice and build LP positions across private markets.

Originally from Stockholm, Benjamin studied in London and spent a decade in finance, marketing and communications with clients including KPMG and HSBC, before establishing BEV.

Today the focus is increasingly on funds and LP positions, building on an earlier track record of direct early-stage investing — four exits and one IPO across food, fintech, climate and consumer.
