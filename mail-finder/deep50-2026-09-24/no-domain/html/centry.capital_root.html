Tax & Wealth

Tax and wealth, connected.

Your tax position shapes your wealth. Your wealth shapes your tax. Centry reviews them as one, so everything is cohesively optimised around what actually matters: achieving your life goals.

Tax

Structure your tax position properly.

For globally mobile people, tax is not just one return in one country. It is residency, company ownership, income, assets, timing, documentation, filings and implementation working together. Reducing unnecessary tax drag will arguably have the highest impact on growing your wealth over time.

Do you replace my existing advisers?

It depends on what works best for you. Many clients choose Centry as their single point of coordination for tax and wealth. Others prefer to keep trusted advisers in place and have Centry work alongside them, providing the central operating layer that connects everything into one plan.

Either way, nothing is disrupted without your agreement.

Do I need to move my assets to work with Centry?

No. Centry starts by understanding what you already own, where it is held, how it is taxed, who advises on it and what decisions are coming up. If a better structure, custodian, portfolio setup or liquidity route is appropriate, Centry can help implement that with the client's authority and the right professional or regulated partner involvement.

Is Centry only for ultra-wealthy families?

No. Centry is for people whose financial life has become too complex for disconnected advisers and spreadsheets. That often means globally mobile founders, investors and families with cross-border tax, residency, company, property, private investment, digital asset, estate planning or adviser complexity. Is Centry regulated?

Yes. Centry operates as a tied agent regulated under the CMVM (Comissão do Mercado de Valores Mobiliários) in Portugal. We are subject to applicable EU regulatory frameworks including MiFID II, MiCAR and ESMA guidelines, and can serve clients across the broader European Union.

Can I use tax services without wealth services?

Yes. Many clients start with tax restructuring alone: residency planning, cross-border filings, entity structuring or compliance review. The wealth and tax services are designed to work together, but you are not required to use both. You can add wealth services later if and when it makes sense.

What happens after the first call?

From there, Centry prepares a detailed tax plan, supports implementation and provides ongoing monitoring so tax and wealth decisions continue to work together.
