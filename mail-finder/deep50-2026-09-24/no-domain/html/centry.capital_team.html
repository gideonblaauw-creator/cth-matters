Centry was built by people who lived the problem. A team of founders, engineers, tax planners, actuaries, investment managers and advisors who couldn't find the joined-up service they needed, so they built it.

Our story

Built from first-hand experience.

Centry was born out of frustration, in Singapore, not Silicon Valley. Brad had scaled startups, worked in finance, and lived across multiple continents. But when it came time to manage his own wealth, spanning assets, jurisdictions, and tax regimes, the system failed him. Every advisor had one piece of the puzzle, but none could connect the whole picture.

So he started building. A system that could unify tax and wealth into a single intelligent strategy. Then he moved to Cascais, and met Rupert, a founder wrestling with the exact same issue. Through YC's cofounder matching network, they teamed up. What started as parallel frustration turned into shared conviction.

Now joined by a seasoned team of operators, technologists, tax planners and investment professionals, Centry is the product they all needed, and couldn't find.
