Our Story

Our Story

Our Story

Centry was born out of frustration — in Singapore, not Silicon Valley.


Brad had scaled startups, worked in finance, and lived across multiple continents. But when it came time to manage his own wealth — spanning assets, jurisdictions, and tax regimes — the system failed him. Every advisor had one piece of the puzzle, but none could connect the whole picture.


So he started building. A system that could unify tax and wealth into a single intelligent strategy.


Then he moved to Cascais — and met Rupert, a founder wrestling with the exact same issue, using ChatGPT to try and hack together a solution. Through YC's cofounder matching network, they teamed up. What started as parallel frustration turned into shared conviction.

Now joined by a seasoned team of operators and technologists, Centry is the product they all needed — and couldn't find.
