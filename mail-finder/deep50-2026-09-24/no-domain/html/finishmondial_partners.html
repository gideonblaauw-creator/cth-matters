From Toilet to Circular Value

“Transforming sanitation systems into sustainable circular ecosystems that generate economic, social, and environmental value for communities worldwide.”

The Investor Problem

Investors face three persistent barriers in sanitation:

Bankability

Few pipelines with steady cash flows and credible repayment histories.

Impact Durability

Difficult to prove outcomes beyond outputs.

Scale

Markets remain fragmented, subsidy-dependent, and hard to replicate.

Our Solution

FINISH is a market architect. FINISH designs the full system so that sanitation becomes investable:

1

Demand

Households are made aware, mobilised, latent demand becomes actual demand.

2

Local SMEs

Trained to deliver to BOP market (high quality, low price) ready to scale.

3

Finance

Financial institutions offer products (literacy, saving and credit) including sanitation loans with low default risk (proven)

4

Circularity

Faecal sludge converted into fertiliser and energy for revenue.

5

Assurance

Data flows track installations, repayments, and outcomes.
