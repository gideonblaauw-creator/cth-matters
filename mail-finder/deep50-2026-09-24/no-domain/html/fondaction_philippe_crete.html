Philippe Crête

Investment Managing Director - Natural Capital, Climate Change and Environmental Markets

Philippe Crête has more than 17 years of experience in climate finance and natural capital. As Investment Managing Director at Fondaction Asset Management (FAM), he develops, raises and manages investment funds in the areas of climate change and natural capital. He manages the Inlandsis, Urapi and Fonds Économie circulaire (Circular Economy Fund) platforms on behalf of FAM. He is also called upon to represent the organization at events related to carbon markets and the financing of nature in the forestry and agricultural sectors. Philippe Crête worked for the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO) for eight years. In this role, he has served as a climate finance expert in more than 20 emerging countries, where he established financial mechanisms to combat deforestation and enable payment for ecosystem services. He was also a partner and deputy director of the ÉcoRessources consultancy firm, which is a pioneer in carbon markets and a forerunner of Coop Carbone. He holds a Bachelor’s degree in Economics and International Development Studies and a Master’s degree in Bioresource Engineering from McGill University.
