Our values

The principles and convictions of how we work, the DNA of NatureRe and the qualities we strive for every day.

RePresent
ReCreate
ReLiable
ReGenerate
RePresent

 

We represent a new way of investing. We believe in our purpose to restore nature and remove carbon at scale with sound returns. We represent a forward-thinking approach to combine the tools of finance with nature restoration. We deeply care about mother earth and push towards a sustainable planet for our future generations.

ReCreate

 

We think outside the box. We create next generation nature-based solutions for impact-minded investors and corporates.  We want to recreate the appreciation for the planet through nature-based impact investing. We look for the best solutions, eager to learn from each other and always remaining open-minded.

ReLiable

 

We strive to build on the highest standards, being a trustworthy partner for all stakeholders and ensuring reliability every step of the way. We are dependable and deliver on our commitments. We take pride in our work and our vision and feel responsible for the collective success.

ReGenerate

 

We stand for the responsible use of our world’s resources. We try to leverage, redirect and optimize what already exists. We use the existing tools of finance and direct them towards nature restoration. We select, protect and restore lands, accelerating natural regrowth processes and helping to regenerate nature.

Destin Whitehurst

Destin is a specialist in channeling capital into high-impact natural climate solutions. Since 2020, he has led investments into early-stage carbon projects across Latin America, East Africa, and Southeast Asia, structuring deals from $5M to $40M in reforestation, mangroves, and agroforestry. With a decade of consulting experience and advisory work for governments and UNDP on biodiversity financing, he brings both financial and policy expertise. At NatureRe, Destin leads the investment team and strengthens investor relations to scale high-quality carbon investments.

Marie-Sophie Teyssier

Marie-Sophie is a global leader in marketing, brand building, and sustainability strategy. She spent 20+ years in senior roles at Nestlé and Zurich Insurance, where she reshaped Zurich’s global brand and communication platform, before dedicating the last five years to impact investing and sustainability initiatives. At NatureRe, Marie-Sophie leverages her international experience to position the company as the global leader in natural climate finance.

Olivier Kobel

Olivier is an expert in structuring and financing sustainable businesses and projects. With more than 20 years of international experience in M&A and corporate finance, he has established a strong reputation in Switzerland’s sustainable finance community. At NatureRe, Olivier supports investment structuring, fundraising, and corporate development to ensure long-term growth and impact.

Marco Meli

Marco is a seasoned investment management executive with deep expertise in fund structuring, operations, and compliance. Over the past 20 years, he has held senior leadership roles at Signet Group, E.I.M. S.A., and UBS Wealth Management, successfully managing multi-jurisdictional platforms and investment vehicles. As COO/CFO at NatureRe, Marco ensures robust operational, financial, and governance foundations for scaling the business.

At NatureRe, Eliana leads nature-based carbon removal projects from project design through monitoring, reporting, and verification.

At NatureRe, Mario leads the implementation of restoration projects.

At NatureRe, Lorraine ensures smooth administrative operations, supporting initiatives that advance sustainable practices and organizational excellence.

Throughout his career, he has managed the acquisition of property rights for major infrastructure projects of national significance across the majority of Colombia, including the departments of Casanare, Arauca, Antioquia, and Santander. At NatureRe, he serves as the Land Acquisition Coordinator, where he leverages his extensive regional expertise and strong social focus to identify, evaluate, and secure the viable land areas necessary to scale the company’s restoration efforts.
