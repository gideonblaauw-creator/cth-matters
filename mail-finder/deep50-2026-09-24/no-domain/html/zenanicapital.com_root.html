We back the builders of Latin America's future.

Zenani Capital is a venture capital firm that partners with visionary, impact-oriented entrepreneurs building the future of Latin America. We provide early-stage capital and operational expertise to startups solving the region's most pressing challenges.

We are passionate about the intersection of technology and impact in three core verticals: Climatetech, Fintech, and Healthtech.

Our mission is to be a vital early-stage institutional partner for LatAm impact founders, offering capital, technical assistance, ecosystem support, and operational expertise through a deep network of regional and global experts — strengthening the pipeline of the LatAm ecosystem, catalyzing follow-on investment, and increasing the success rate of impact-driven companies.
