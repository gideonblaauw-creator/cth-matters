WHO WE ARE

i80 Group is a private ABF investment firm focused on delivering flexible, asset-based capital to leading companies. We specialize in providing structured credit solutions to businesses with strong fundamentals and scalable models—enabling them to unlock value, accelerate growth, and reach their next stage of development.

 

Our approach is grounded in institutional rigor, thoughtful structuring, and long-term partnership. We underwrite with discipline, move with speed, and prioritize alignment above all else.

 

The i80 team brings decades of experience across private credit, investment management and capital markets. Since our founding, we have committed over $3 billion in capital across a diverse and growing portfolio, and we remain committed to supporting high-quality businesses with tailored, reliable capital.

WHAT WE DO

i80 Group provides tailored, asset-based credit solutions to companies and platforms. We partner with businesses at pivotal moments by structuring capital that is flexible, scalable, and aligned. Our solutions range from senior credit facilities to programmatic capital commitments.

 

We focus on asset-backed finance across sectors where traditional capital can be rigid or unavailable. Our deep structuring expertise and ability to understand both the underlying assets and business models allows us to create financing that supports growth while managing risk.

 

We work directly with companies, as well as with sponsors and platforms, to design and deliver capital solutions that go beyond the limitations of conventional financing.

 

Our commitment is to be responsive, reliable, and relationship-driven—bringing a credit partner mindset to every engagement.
