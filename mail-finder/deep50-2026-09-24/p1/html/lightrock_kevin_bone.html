Kevin Bone

Investment Team, Partner

Kevin joined Lightrock in 2016. Prior to Lightrock, Kevin spent more than 20 years in technology, principally as an investor across growth and venture stages globally, but previously in CEO roles leading restructurings and turnarounds of three businesses, as an investment banker, and as a consultant.

Kevin holds a BA in Japanese & Economics from Sheffield University, a Diploma in Aid Economics from Chuo University and qualified as a Chartered Accountant. He has been an FCA regulated individual since 1996.
