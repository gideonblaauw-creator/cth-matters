Senior Team

Lendable’s people bring deep experience from the worlds of asset management, finance, fintech, ESG, impact investment, microfinance and more. We are united by a desire to make a difference. To grow fintech companies in emerging and frontier markets. To unleash the economic potential of people and businesses. To deliver consistent and compelling investment returns.

Together, we are Lendable.

Diversity, culture and career

Lendable is built by the people who work here.
