Sobre la institución

Impacta VC invests in impact startups in Latin America. Impacta VC launched a U$7MM Seed Fund in the US to fund tech companies aligned with the SDGs across LATAM that leverage the partner’s network of top regional founders and VCs to achieve future financing rounds in the impact space.
