About

Eran is based in Silicon Valley and leads J-Impact a global venture fund that is based on the value of Tikun Olam and is out to produce a double bottom line - financial AND a social/environmental impact.

‍

J-Impact is part of the J-Ventures group and includes a capitalist kibbutz over 400 investors across 30 cities who are leaders in venture, corporates, startups and philanthropy.

‍

Eran and the team invest in and empower early stage startups that are out to impact the world in the areas of health, climate, food and social equity.

‍

Eran is focused on the development of human awareness and maximizing human potential since 2018.

‍

Prior to J-Impact, Eran led startups inside global Fortune 500 companies in the fields of communication, IOT, autonomous vehicles and connected services, advised startups and corporations on strategy and corporate development and been active in multiple early stage and late stage funds.

‍

Eran has started a group and been active in the last 5 years in creating jobs in the Palestinian tech sector through collaboration with Israeli companies.
