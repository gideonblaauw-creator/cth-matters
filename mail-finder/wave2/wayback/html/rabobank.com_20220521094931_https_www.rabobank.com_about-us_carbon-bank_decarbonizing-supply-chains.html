Decarbonizing Supply Chains

Achieving emission reductions in Food and Agri supply chains by incentivizing partners to take action. We help you achieve, monitor, verify and monetize the carbon reduction of your farmers and suppliers to reduce your Scope 3 emissions and help farms become more resilient.

Get the support you need

We work with a range of partners from project scoping to implementation, so you and your suppliers get the needed support to make supply chain decarbonization a reality.

Achieve Science-based results

We strive for accurate results based on scientifically proved methods. We follow the Value Chains Interventions Guidance and Science Based Targets initiative recommendations.

Monetize Carbon Reduction

We help you monetize carbon reduction and provide incentive models so farmers and suppliers can implement needed lower-carbon practices.

Empower your farmers

Rabobank intends to pre-pay farmers to transition to low-carbon practices based on calculated carbon reduction, which enables them to implement the necessary practices and become more resilient in mid and long term.

Based on the data collected we create a baseline measurement on farm level

We support the implementation of practices and changes at farm level

8
Verification and monitoring

Using science-based methodologies we quantify the reduction results

9
Reduction and removal allocation and claims

We help with the allocation of claims and reporting on changes
