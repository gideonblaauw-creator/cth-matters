Buy verified nature‑based carbon credits - backed by Rabobank

Rabo Carbon Connect offers high-quality nature-based carbon credits with the aim to help companies with their ambitions to responsibly achieve CO₂ compensation while supporting verified climate projects. With trusted standards like Gold Standard, Verra, and Plan Vivo, we try to make it easy for businesses in the Netherlands and beyond to buy carbon credits with confidence. We simplify the process, offer transparent pricing, and guide you through the complexities, aiming for the additionality, permanence, and measurable impact.

Why Clients Choose Rabo Carbon Connect

Organizations trust choose Rabo Carbon Connect because we combine Rabobank’s 5 year expertise and extensive network in the voluntary carbon market. We source high-quality, nature-based carbon credits verified under Gold Standard, Verra, and Plan Vivo. Every credit undergoes strict checks for additionality, permanence, and social safeguards.

What makes us different?

    Transparent pricing and clear documentation for compliance. Due diligence on every project, including registry IDs and retirement proofs. Expert guidance in the form of training sessions at no additional cost.

Who benefits from Rabo Carbon Connect?

We support companies committed those that prioritize emission reductions first and use carbon credits only for residual emissions. Our typical clients include:

    Food and agriculture businesses seeking nature-based solutions. Logistics and manufacturing firms with hard-to-abate emissions. Corporates aiming for SBTi-aligned net-zero strategies.

If you want to offset responsibly, comply with EU green claims guidance, and contribute to verified climate projects, we are here to help.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Voluntary carbon credits represent one metric ton of CO₂ equivalent reduced or removed from the atmosphere through certified projects. Companies use them to compensate for residual emissions after serious reduction efforts, supporting climate goals and stakeholder expectations. Rabobank focuses on nature-based credits from top registries, with the aim to ensure integrity and impact.

Explore Rabobank’s curated portfolio of high-quality credits.

By looking for credits from projects rated BBB or higher under recognized integrity frameworks and aligned with ICVCM Core Carbon Principles. VCMI provides guidance for credible corporate claims. Rabobank only sources credits that meet these standards, so your climate claims remain future-proof.

A BBB rating (from BeZero) signals strong adherence to quality benchmarks—covering additionality, permanence, and robust monitoring. It reduces reputational and compliance risks compared to unrated or low-rated projects. BeZero is an independent carbon credit ratings agency that evaluates the likelihood that a voluntary carbon credit truly represents one tonne of CO₂e avoided or removed. Its ratings are widely used by buyers, investors, and intermediaries to assess credit integrity beyond registry certification.

Nature-based solutions—such as reforestation, agroforestry, and wetland restoration— aim to deliver co-benefits like biodiversity, water security, and community livelihoods. They also align with corporate ESG and SDG commitments. We prioritize nature-based carbon credits currently because they align with our deep roots in food and agriculture. This heritage gives us unique expertise in projects like reforestation and agroforestry, enhancing the chances of ensuring quality and impact of these projects. Beyond carbon removal, nature-based solutions aim to deliver co-benefits—such as biodiversity, soil health, and community resilience—that in our view technology-based approaches often cannot match.

Leading registries include Verra (VCS), Gold Standard, and Climate Action Reserve (CAR), known for rigorous methodologies and transparent governance. The Rabo Carbon Bank only sources credits from project developers that issue credits under reputable registries.

Every client’s approach to carbon offsetting is unique, but we often see a common pattern: building high-integrity portfolios that complement strong reduction strategies. This typically involves a mix of cost-effective avoidance credits and premium-quality removals.

Corresponding adjustments ensure credits are not double-counted under national inventories, a key consideration for companies seeking Paris Agreement alignment. While not mandatory for all voluntary claims, they are increasingly relevant for global leaders. The Rabo Carbon Bank monitors policy developments to keep your strategy aligned.

High-quality nature-based projects aim to deliver measurable co-benefits—such as poverty reduction, gender equality, and biodiversity conservation—helping companies advance ESG and SDG agendas alongside climate targets.

Common risks include low-quality credits, lack of transparency, fraud and future claim misalignment. These can lead to reputational damage and stranded assets. The Rabo Carbon Bank aims to mitigate these risks through applying strict quality filters to the projects that get onboarded to the portfolio.
