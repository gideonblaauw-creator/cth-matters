The TURN8 seed accelerator

TURN8 seed accelerator program is designed to encourage innovative entrepreneurship worldwide, starting with Dubai. We look for people with ingenious ideas that can be refined and brought to market through a “seed accelerator”, a program that selects start-up teams with marketable ideas and supports them with funding, mentoring and training in exchange for a stake in any resulting business.
TURN8 holds two rounds a year, beginning with seeking innovative ideas through an online campaign and events in select countries. This is followed by a 120-day funded seed accelerator where teams perfect their business model and develop a Minimum Viable Product. For those who graduate, follow-on support is offered to help them find additional investors.

TURN8 program to bring innovative start-up ideas to market.
TURN8 is an initiative launched by DP World to foster a global entrepreneurial and innovation culture. The aim is to inject entrepreneurial know-how and accelerate commercialise-able “ideas to market. ”This revolutionary program will strategically scout for international early stage ideas and concepts that can be refined and brought to market through the TURN8 seed accelerator.
The program will take place over 12 months, beginning with a rigorous online and event-based international scouting campaign, followed by a 120-day funded “Rapid Fire” Business Modelling and Prototyping seed accelerator, with extensive follow-on support. This program is a remarkable collaboration between enterprise and innovators.
