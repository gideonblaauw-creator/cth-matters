ABOUT US

We are a Venture Capital firm that invests in early-stage startups, cofounded by extraordinary teams, that are scalable, have solid economic fundamentals, and are connected to Latin America.

Our purpose is to empower entrepreneurs to reach their full potential and, through technology, reshape the future of Latin America. We offer not just capital, but also connections and knowledge to support their growth.

SIMMA VENTURES

Industry-agnostic, focused on Latin America, investing mainly in seed rounds and as co-investors.

BIOS VENTURES

Specializes in animal tech, the pet industry, agtech, foodtech, sustainability, and advanced manufacturing. It is the CVC arm of Grupo BIOS, the largest agro-industrial company in Colombia.

INVX

An angel investment fund for pre-seed startups in Colombia.

We contribute value to our portfolio startups by assisting them with fundraising efforts and facilitating business connections.
