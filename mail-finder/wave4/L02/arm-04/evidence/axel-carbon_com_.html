Accelerating Decarbonization

We back the pioneers re-engineering industries for a faster, decarbonized future. We fuel Europe's top early-stage Climate Tech founders. 

Discover Our Strategy 
Who we are

We are a pan-European climate tech investment holding company, backed by a high-profile collective of angel investors, industry experts, and single family offices.

We are united by one mission: to accelerate decarbonization by building a high-impact portfolio and backing the pioneers tackling the climate challenge.

our approach

Our approach is built on strategic conviction and deep expertise. We invest portfolio-driven to maximize both climate impact and financial returns.

With roots in southern Europe, we cover multiple regions and expertise, providing founders a network of knowledge and connections.

STRATEGY

We focus on tangible, hardware-first technologies with measurable climate KPIs, acting as strategic co-investors alongside top-tier VC partners.
