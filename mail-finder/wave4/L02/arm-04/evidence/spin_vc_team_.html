Team

Spin Ventures has built a team specifically designed to execute our two pillars: Venture Capital investment and the House of Circularity open innovation platform. We bring together diverse expertise in venture investment, climate tech, corporate innovation, and circular economy transformation, all under one roof.

Our team’s combined skills cover early-stage venture development, growth acceleration, and corporate strategy, enabling us to effectively support both portfolio companies and corporate partners. This approach ensures we can scale solutions, de-risk investments, and drive measurable, profitable outcomes in the transition to a circular and regenerative economy.

We are supported by a strong group of advisors with extensive experience in consumer industries, technology, and investment, ensuring that Spin Ventures is uniquely positioned to accelerate growth, mitigate risks, and unlock the full potential of circularity.
