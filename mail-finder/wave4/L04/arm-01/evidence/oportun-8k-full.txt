 <p style="margin-top:0pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman;font-weight:bold"><span style="font-style:italic"><span style="text-decoration:underline">Item</span><span style="text-decoration:underline">&#8201;</span><span style="text-decoration:underline">2.03. Creation of a Direct Financial Obligation or an Obligation under an <span style="white-space:nowrap">Off-Balance</span> Sheet Arrangement of a Registrant</span> </span></p> <p style="margin-top:6pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman"><span style="font-style:italic">Personal Loan Warehouse Facility (PLW IV) </span></p> <p style="margin-top:6pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">On October&#160;14, 2025, Oportun Financial Corporation (the &#8220;Company&#8221;) entered into a new warehouse financing facility (the &#8220;PLW IV Warehouse Facility&#8221;). In connection with the PLW IV Warehouse Facility, Oportun PLW IV Trust (the &#8220;Borrower&#8221;), a subsidiary of the Company, entered into a Loan and Security Agreement (the &#8220;Loan and Security Agreement&#8221;) with certain lenders from time to time party thereto (the &#8220;Lenders&#8221;), Wilmington Trust, National Association as collateral agent, administrative agent, paying agent, securities intermediary and depositary bank (&#8220;Wilmington Trust&#8221;). The PLW IV Warehouse Facility has a three-year term and a borrowing capacity of approximately $247&#160;million. </p> <p style="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">Borrowings under the Loan and Security Agreement accrue interest at an interest rate no greater than Term SOFR plus a weighted average spread up to 2.58%. The advance rate for the PLW IV Warehouse Facility is 95.0%, subject to certain default, delinquency and liquidity triggers that could lower the advance rate to 92.0%. </p> <p style="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">The Loan and Security Agreement includes customary representations and warranties, as well as affirmative and negative covenants, including certain financial maintenance covenants. These covenants require the Company and its subsidiaries to not exceed a specified leverage ratio, to maintain a minimum tangible net worth, and to maintain a minimum level of unrestricted cash or cash equivalents while any borrowings under the Loan and Security Agreement are outstanding. The Loan and Security Agreement also contains customary events of default. <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">The offering included five classes of fixed rate notes: Class&nbsp;A, Class&nbsp;B, Class&nbsp;C, Class&nbsp;D, and Class&nbsp;E. Fitch rated all classes of

STYLE="white-space:nowrap">co-leads.</FONT> </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">The weighted average coupon on the transaction was 5.69%, and the weighted average yield was 5.77%. The
Class&nbsp;A notes were priced with a coupon of 4.53% per annum; the Class&nbsp;B notes were priced with a coupon of 5.31% per annum; the Class&nbsp;C notes were priced with a coupon of 5.80% per annum; the Class&nbsp;D notes were priced with a
coupon of 6.97% per annum; and the Class&nbsp;E notes were priced with a coupon of 10.82% per annum. </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">&#8220;This was Oportun&#8217;s fourth ABS

consistently raise <FONT STYLE="white-space:nowrap">low-cost</FONT> sources of capital to fund our operations in service of our members.&#8221; </P> <P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">For more
