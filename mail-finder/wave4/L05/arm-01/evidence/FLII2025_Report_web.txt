FLII is the leading gathering for impact investment and 
social entrepreneurship in Latin America. Since its 
founding in 2011, FLII has brought together investors, 
entrepreneurs, corporations, government representatives, 
and ecosystem builders to drive innovative solutions to the 
region’s most pressing social and environmental 
challenges.
Each year, FLII curates a dynamic agenda featuring 
thought-provoking panels, interactive workshops, pitch 
sessions, and curated networking spaces that empower 
attendees to move from ideas to concrete actions. With a 
strong focus on mobilizing capital for impact, the event 
serves as a catalyst for scaling transformative solutions in 
areas such as climate resilience, ﬁnancial inclusion, gender 
equality, and sustainable business models.
As we approach critical milestones for global development, 
FLII remains committed to igniting action and 
strengthening the impact ecosystem—one conversation, 
one investment, and one bold initiative at a time.
Celebrating its 15th edition in 2025, FLII has established 
itself as a platform for collaboration, 
knowledge-sharing, and action, fostering high-impact 
partnerships that shape the future of sustainable 
development in Latin America.

One of the most anticipated traditions of FLII, the 
Investors’ Drinks, once again provided a dynamic and 
interactive way for entrepreneurs and investors to 
connect. In this unique format, investors step behind 
the bar to craft cocktails, while entrepreneurs pitch 
their ventures within the time it takes to make a 
drink. This engaging and informal setting fosters 
genuine conversations and new investment 
opportunities, breaking down traditional barriers 
between investors and founders.
The evening featured a traditional Yucatecan feast, 
curated and prepared by Doña Miriam, a renowned 
local chef celebrated for preserving the region’s 
authentic culinary heritage. Guests enjoyed iconic 
dishes such as cochinita pibil, lechon, and handmade 
tortillas, immersing themselves in the rich ﬂavors of 
Yucatán while strengthening connections in an 
atmosphere of warmth and community.
With an inspiring start, the FLII opening ceremony set 
the tone for three days of meaningful discussions, bold 
ideas, and decisive actions, reinforcing the event’s 
mission to accelerate impact across Latin America.

through an immersive meditation designed to deepen their connection with 
nature and inspire visions of a regenerative future. Rooted in the principles of

grand tree in the hacienda, the session fostered deep emotional connection, 
self-expression, and community through shared laughter, screams, and moments 
of profound realization.

This year, FLII introduced a new format called FLII 
Talks - Ideas in movement,  a dynamic 15-minute 
keynote sessions designed to spotlight critical 
topics shaping the impact ecosystem. 
These powerful talks featured inﬂuential leaders sharing insights, bold ideas, 
and innovative solutions in a concise and engaging format. From visionary 
investors to pioneering entrepreneurs, FLII Talks provided a platform for in-depth 
reﬂections on the challenges and opportunities in impact investing, 
sustainability, and social innovation. This new addition brought fresh energy to 
the agenda, allowing attendees to gain valuable perspectives in a fast-paced, 
thought-provoking setting.
Raúl Pomares, founder of Sonen Capital, delivered a powerful FLII Talk at FLII 2025, 
emphasizing the urgent need to build a resilient capital system for Latin America 
in the face of global uncertainty. He highlighted how recent crises, including the 
freeze of $2.3 billion in USAID funds, have exposed the fragility of traditional 
funding systems and the necessity of mobilizing private capital to bridge these 
gaps. Through examples like Sistema.bio and Betterﬂy, Pomares illustrated how 
impact investments can generate ﬁnancial returns while addressing critical issues 
such as food security and access to healthcare. He also called for a redeﬁnition of 
success, stressing that social and environmental impact should be valued as 
much as ﬁnancial proﬁtability. Pomares underscored the importance of 
collaboration among entrepreneurs, investors, and fund managers to strengthen 
the impact ecosystem in the region. Concluding his talk, he urged attendees to act 
with urgency and commitment, reminding them that transforming ﬁnancial 
systems and developing sustainable solutions require collective action.

As we close the 15th edition of the Foro Latinoamericano de Inversión de 
Impacto, we’re reminded of the urgency—and the power—of collective action. 
FLII 2025 was more than a gathering; it was a rallying call to move from vision 
to execution, from intention to measurable impact.
Under the theme “Countdown: Actions into Motion,” this year’s FLII brought 
together changemakers from across Latin America and beyond—investors, 
entrepreneurs, policymakers, and ecosystem builders—who came not just to 
discuss, but to act. Over the course of three days, we saw bold ideas take shape, 
new partnerships form, and a shared commitment emerge: to build a more 
sustainable, inclusive, and resilient future for our region.
With action tracks focused on climate and biodiversity, social equity, and 
technology for impact, FLII 2025 challenged us all to move faster and work 
smarter. We celebrated innovation, recognized leadership, and held space for 
critical conversations that will shape the next chapter of our ecosystem.
As we look ahead, the countdown continues. The path forward will demand 
courage, collaboration, and relentless dedication. But if FLII 2025 showed us 
anything, it’s that when this community comes together with purpose, 
transformative change is not only possible—it’s already in motion.
Thank you for being part of this milestone edition. Let’s carry this 
momentum forward. The time to act is now.
