Susie Larson

KEYNOTE Speaker

Susie Larson | Susie is a popular radio talk show host with over 7 million podcast downloads, a national speaker, and a bestselling author. Author of twenty-two books, Susie is also a veteran in the fitness field and has twice been voted a top-ten finalist for the John C. Maxwell Transformational Leadership Award.

Susie and her husband live near Minneapolis with their three sons, three daughters-in-law, and a growing bunch of delightful grandchildren. Her passion is to see people everywhere awakened to the value of their soul, the depth of God’s love, and the height of their calling in Christ Jesus.

Learn more at SusieLarson.com

Topic:

Remember God

The Israelites were perpetual forgetters. So are we. If we forget to remember God, we’ll forget who we are. If we forget God, we’ll tell our stories incorrectly. But when we remember God, when we rehearse His faithfulness and tell of His mighty deeds, our faith is strengthened and we are changed. If you’re in between miracles, borrow one from scripture. He’s the same God—yesterday, today, and forever.

Psalm 77:11-12 ~ “I will remember the deeds of the Lord; yes, I will remember your wonders of old. I will ponder all your work, and meditate on your mighty deeds.”

More Than You Can Imagine

Scripture reminds us that God is able to do exceedingly abundantly above and beyond all we could ever dare to ask or imagine… He wants to, He can do it, and He will do it! But the clincher lies in the next word in this verse: according. According to His work within us. God wants to work wonders in and through us. The question is: how much access will we give Him to our story? It’s time to build a more resilient trust in God. He’s worth it, and when we trust Him, we are changed from the inside out.

Ephesians 3:20-21 ~ Now all glory to God, who is able, through his mighty power at work within us, to accomplish infinitely more than we might ask or think. 21 Glory to him in the church and in Christ Jesus through all generations forever and ever! Amen.

Topic: The Heart of Delight

Description: The Bible says that God will “take great delight in you.” But what does that mean? We all have stories and examples of reasons the Creator of the Universe would not take delight in us. Sometimes our sinful nature can cloud our understanding of why God would even want to take delight in us. So how can this be? How can a perfect God delight in an imperfect sinner like me? Let’s explore God’s Word through the Prophet Zephaniah to discover God’s heart of restoration, forgiveness, and love and how we can rest in the assurance of this delight.

Topic: Once Upon a Time … Then Life Happened

Description: Does your life story ever feel as though the plot developed a mind of its own, the people you love stopped following the script, and you somehow became responsible for keeping the whole cast together? Take heart. You were never meant to manage every character, fix every scene, or figure out how the story ends. God delights in you. The real you. The woman living this chapter, loving imperfect people, making imperfect choices, and learning as she goes. Jesus keeps saying, “Come. Tera Lind

Keynote Speaker

Tera stepped into full-time ministry ten years ago after working professionally in the Marketing and Business field. She continued her education by earning her master’s degree at Moody Theological Seminary in 2016. Tera is relatable and is known for her ability to warm up a crowd and make them feel welcome and encouraged along with making them laugh. Because of her broken past, she has a heart of empathy and grace for people in all different seasons of life. She believes that being authentic and vulnerable are key to building relationships of trust, especially in hard topics within the church. Workshop Topic:Whose Voice Are You Believing?

Description:

Have you ever walked away from a conversation wondering if you said too much, or not enough? Replayed a mistake from years ago? Felt crushed by the fear of disappointing someone or constantly questioning if you’re enough? The voices of shame, fear, perfectionism, and people-pleasing can become so familiar that we mistake them for truth.

In this session, we’ll learn how to recognize the voices competing for our attention and discover how to tune our hearts to the voice of Jesus. Together, we’ll explore what Scripture says about taking our thoughts captive, silencing the accuser, and living from our identity in Christ instead of the opinions of others. You don’t have to live exhausted by the pressure to be everything for everyone. Jesus is inviting you to exchange the noise for His truth, and to experience the freedom that comes from believing His voice above all others.

Elizabeth Nejedlo

Tile:  FORGED

Description:

In Forged, Elizabeth invites women into the powerful story of Jacob’s life-changing encounter with God and explores how some of God’s greatest work happens in the very places we feel weakest. Joy Kapheim

Speaker : Chapel 2:15 pm

She is a devoted wife, mother of two, mother-in-law to one and a new grandmother who brings both real-world experience and deep spiritual insight to every stage she steps on. Having spent years in the marketplace working with large corporations, small businesses, and as a business owner herself, she understands the pressures, risks, and rewards that come with building something meaningful. Topic: Who and where Is God in the Waiting?

Description: Waiting seasons often leave us asking questions, wondering when God will move, and struggling to trust His timing. Through personal stories, biblical truth, and practical lessons learned in the midst of uncertainty, Joy explores not only who God reveals Himself to be in the waiting, but who He is shaping us to become and practical ways hold fast to faith while in the thick of it all. This message will encourage you to recognize God’s faithfulness, discover purpose in the pause, and find hope while you wait for what comes next.

Topic:Know Your Enemy

Description: Satan is at work in the world—this is his domain and we can see the obvious evils. But he is also
at work trying to get believers to be tempted to doubt God and he uses deceptive snares to get
believers out of the will of God. We have to be on our guard, vigilant and alert, watching for his
schemes. We need to know our enemy and have strategies for fighting him. We will learn who
he is, how to recognize where and when Satan is at work, and strategies to combat this enemy.
So many of us feel anxiety, worry, fear, depression and stress; but these are the result of the
enemy’s tactics. We will look at how to restore the hope, joy and victorious strength that God
provides, and how God delights in us when we rely on Him to overcome the enemy.

Description: As women, we often feel called to serve everyone, yet struggle to give ourselves permission to slow down and rest. Sarah Schneider

Online Exclusive Speaker

Sarah Schneider didn’t discover her message in a seminary, but in a small visiting room at her local jail. As a volunteer mentor with Fox Valley Jail Ministries for over a decade, she sat across from incarcerated women whose desperate cravings for destructive substances mirrored her own hunger for God and His Word. That realization sparked her passion and her ministry: helping women who deeply wanted their lives to change but didn’t know where to start.

Ever since, Sarah has been passionately guiding women to break destructive cycles—whether substances, shopping, doomscrolling, sugar, or anything that promises happiness but leaves you empty—by cultivating the one healthy addiction: God Himself. She loves giving practical, foundational guidance to anyone desiring a deeper, more intimate relationship with God and His Word.

She hosts the Addicted to God podcast, writes at GodlyGirlfriends.com since 2013, and leads women’s Bible studies and a support group at Alliance Church Appleton; her church home for more than twenty years. Sarah and her husband live in Appleton, Wisconsin. In addition to writing, teaching, and volunteering, she works full-time and enjoys coffee, reading, traveling, time with family, her four dogs, and being outdoors in God’s creation.

Topic: Bible 101: Where Do I Start?

Description: Feeling overwhelmed by the Bible or unsure where to even begin? In this practical and encouraging breakout, Sarah Annie Schneider will help you see Scripture in a fresh, new light and equip you with simple tips and tools to open God’s Word with confidence and joy. Drawing from her years of leading women’s Bible studies, mentoring women through jail ministry, and her own personal journey, Sarah will share approachable ways to build a sustainable habit of reading Scripture—without guilt or pressure. Whether you’re brand new to the Bible, returning to your faith, or simply longing for a deeper relationship with Jesus, you’ll leave with clear starting points, helpful tools, and renewed excitement to discover the living hope found in God’s Word.
