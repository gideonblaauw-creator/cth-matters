Nic Gorini

Founder & Managing Partner

Spin Ventures

Nic Gorini is the Managing Partner of Spin Ventures, a leader in advancing circularity and decarbonization within the $30 trillion global consumer and retail industries. With over two decades in climate tech, Nic drives Spin Ventures’ mission to accelerate the transition to regenerative economies through its €75 million Loop 2 Fund. This Article 9 "Dark Green" initiative targets pre-seed to Series A startups, focusing on transformative B2B solutions that deliver carbon-negative value chains and closed-loop systems. Under Nic's leadership, Loop 2 invests in four strategic areas: Material Science, Recycling Technologies, SaaS Platforms, and ReCommerce. These investments align with Spin Ventures’ proprietary "House of Circularity" framework, which facilitates unmatched deal flow, pilot validation with FT500 corporations, and risk mitigation through a collaborative ecosystem approach. Nic’s vision ensures that every initiative drives measurable outcomes, balancing environmental impact with financial returns, positioning Spin Ventures at the forefront of the circular economy revolution.
