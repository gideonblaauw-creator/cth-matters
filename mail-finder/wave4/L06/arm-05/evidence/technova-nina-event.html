The Future is female (Demoday)
Date
Thu, 02/12/2026 - 12:00

This special session brings together a powerful lineup of women who were once part of the Technova team and are now active leaders in the entrepreneurial and innovation space. Together, they will reflect on the evolution of our ecosystem and share their visions for a more inclusive, connected, and global future.

Speakers:

  • Gina Vidal, Systemic Coach and Leadership Consultant at Gina Vidal

  • Meritxell Bassoles, Strategic Project Officer at Computer Vision Center

  • Nina Alastruey, Entrepreneur and VC Investor

  • Chairwoman: Carmen Adán, Manager La Salle Technova Barcelona

Join us for an inspiring conversation that celebrates the past while looking boldly ahead.
