We live and breathe tech. Photos created with AI by Black Forest Labs.

Tim joined Earlybird's Berlin office in 2018 to especially expand on the fund’s coverage in Fintech and Web3. Today, he serves on the boards of several high-profile companies, including Upvest, Hive Tech, finmid, Flanks and M^Zero Labs.

Before joining Earlybird, Tim founded a European crypto exchange, serving as its CEO for several years. He also worked as a CFO in Asia for a social gaming venture and gained experience in the Investment Banking Division (M&A) at Jefferies & Company. Tim began his career in financial consultancy, working on various projects with mid-sized German electrical engineering firms.

He studied International Business at the University of Maastricht and holds an MSc in Finance from the University of St Andrews.

I am personally interested in…

advocating the concept of a decentralized version of the internet and run decentralized business applications based on blockchain technology.

My most challenging adventure was…

traveling up the andes on 5,000 meters height from Bolivia to Chile within 5 days.

Hire people who…

think outside the box and know things that you don’t.
