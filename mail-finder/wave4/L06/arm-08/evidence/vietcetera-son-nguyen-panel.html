Supporting SMEs on their Investment Readiness and ESG Impact Journey - 2024 ESG Investor Conference

45:59133 lượt xem
Đã sao chép
SPEAKERS:
- Rong Yu, Head of Greater China & SEA, ESG Solutions, S&P Global Sustainable1​
- Son Nguyen, Associate Vice President, Impact Partners, Impact Investment Exchange (IIX)
- Yen Do, Investment Manager, Beacon Fund
- Le Duy An, Director, Bo Cau Services Company

Moderated by: Xavier Depouilly, General Manager, Indochina Research

Of the 865,000 enterprises operating in Vietnam, about 98% are SMEs and play a critical role in Vietnam’s quest to build a resilient and strong future for its people. How can SMEs tap into the tools and resources they need to access financing and raise their profiles as social and environmental impact businesses? Experts with experience in supporting businesses in starting their ESG journey will share insights on what is required to be investment-ready as well as best practices and tools to support robust ESG and impact measuring.
