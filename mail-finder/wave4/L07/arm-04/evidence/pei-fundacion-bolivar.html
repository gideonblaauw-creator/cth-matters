    Fundación Bolívar Davivienda overview

    Founded in 2009, Fundación Bolívar Davivienda is a non-profit organisation based in Colombia that supports and develops the community through philanthropy.

    This institution is a global alternative asset manager with a multi-decade track record across private markets. The firm invests on behalf of institutional partners through a family of strategies spanning several asset classes and regions. Its teams operate from offices around the world, working with investors and portfolio companies over long holding periods. Subscribe for the full profile, background and analysis.
