About Investor

Aidan Madigan-Curtis is a partner at Eclipse Ventures, where she invests in companies transforming physical industries across deep tech and hardware, logistics, robotics, energy, and health. Eclipse invests from pre-seed through later rounds, and recent fund profiles place its average check size at $200K to $5M. Before joining Eclipse, she helped scale Apple’s manufacturing operations and later rose to vice president and general manager at Samsara.
