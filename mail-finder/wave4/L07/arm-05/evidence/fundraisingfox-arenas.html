About

Alejandro Arenas is a Senior Director on CIM's investment team based in Bogotá, Colombia. He previously worked as a VP handling early-stage venture capital at investment bank Inverlink, as a private equity associate for Latin America at Developing World Markets, and as a private bank analyst at J.P. Morgan. He holds an MBA from Cambridge Judge Business School and a BA in Economics from Universidad de los Andes.
