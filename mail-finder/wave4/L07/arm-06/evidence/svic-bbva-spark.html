BBVA Spark

CorporateFounded 2022Website ↗

BBVA Spark is BBVA’s bank for the tech ecosystem — a division created to help entrepreneurs and venture-capital investors scale with financial services and debt, not a classic GP fund. The official English home says it offers daily banking, venture debt and other credit that underwrites potential rather than only historical financials, plus a dedicated team and access to BBVA’s events and fund investments. Public figures on the same page: 1,800+ customers, €1 billion in available financing, presence in Argentina, Colombia, Spain, Mexico and Europe. Launched in Spain and Mexico in July 2022. Santiago Muguruza is publicly described as leading the unit; that name is not printed on the homepage team block, so it stays in notes.
