Investment thesis

Lightrock invests in technology-enabled companies delivering proven solutions to pressing global challenges, organized around three themes: People, Planet, and Productivity. Its private market strategies focus on growth-stage companies that use technology to create scalable, positive social and environmental impact while also pursuing financial returns. The firm targets sectors offering disproportionate value creation alongside impact opportunities in response to social and environmental challenges. Lightrock positions itself as a thought partner and adviser to portfolio company CEOs, prioritizing their needs. Its client base includes pension funds, charities, asset managers, endowments, family offices, corporates, and sovereign institutions.
