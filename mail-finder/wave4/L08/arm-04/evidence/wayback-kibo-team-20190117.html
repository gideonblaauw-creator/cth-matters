Aquilino Peña

Founding Partner

Aquilino Peña has been involved in the digital industry since the late 90s and has been involved in all areas of the Business, mainly ecommerce, online marketing and content.

Aquilino has been an active Angel investor and advisor to a number of companies such as BaseKit (UK), Acierto.com, Matrix, Bodeboca, PayCo(Germany), Sage Spain (Advisory Comitee Chairman), Alice (US/España) amongst other relevant projects.

His last corporate assignment was as CEO of MediaEdge:cia for Spain and Portugal, the largest media buying agency of WPP in the region, which manages the marketing investment of over 500 clients in Spain and Portugal. Relevant clients included: Sony Ericsson, Chanel, Henkel, Visa, Paramount Pictures, Accenture, Toys R US,… Before that, he ran all portal activities for Orange Spain, including e-care, e-shop and the large portal network managed by Orange in the Spanish language (up to 40 M monthly active users).

Aquilino started his career as a consultant for The Boston Consulting Group, where he worked at the Madrid office and started up the Sao Paulo office. Aquilino is well known and considered a key person in the European Internet arena. He has been Director and member of the board of the European Interactive Advertising Association (EIAA), Chairman of the Interactive Advertising Bureau (IAB-Spain), board member of the Asociacion Española de Anunciantes (AEA) and board member of @adigital (formerly Spanish Ecommerce Association).

Aquilino received an MBA from the Harvard Business School and holds a dual degree in Law and Business Administration from ICADE and is fluent in English, Spanish, French and Portuguese.

Aquilino and Javier were classmates at ICADE and close friends since.

Javier Torremocha

Founding Partner

Javier Torremocha has more than 16 years of transaction experience with leading global firms like Lehman Brothers (1994-2000) and UBS (2001-2009). In UBS he was Head of Corporate Advisory, where he was solely focused in advising entrepreneurs and family owned companies in corporate finance transactions. In between Lehman Brothers and UBS, he worked as Director of Business Development at Netjuice Capital, an internet VC funded by 3i, GE Capital and FCC with commitments over €60 million. Netjuice owned a 15% stake in Kelkoo.com which was sold to Yahoo Inc. for $575 million in 2004. His responsibilities in Netjuice included fundraising activity, M&A and business development at a holding level and for portfolio companies.

In 2009, Javier moved to San Diego, CA, where he took a Global Leadership Program at the University of California San Diego (UCSD) and gain insight of the dynamics of the early stage US VC industry. He is a the only non-US LP of Social Leverage LLC, a San Diego-based partnership that invests in early stage internet businesses. Social Leverage currently holds a portfolio of 20+ companies, including Stocktwits (recently named “one of the top 10 most innovative companies in web”), Ticketfly, Bit.ly and eToro. In addition, Javier has been an active Angel Investor, including companies like Idealista, Mundivox, Pulsar Technologies and Netjuice.

Javier received an MBA from INSEAD and holds a dual degree in Law and Business Administration from ICADE.

Javier and Aquilino were classmates at ICADE and close friends since. Javier and José María worked together for 5 years at UBS and have known each other for over 15 years.

Jose María Amusátegui

Founding Partner

José María Amusátegui is a Founding Partner of Cygnus Asset Management SGIIC SA, the first independent regulated firm approved in Spain to manage hedge funds. He has over 25 years experience in Consulting, Private Equity, Investment and Private Banking. Before joining Cygnus, José María worked for 14 years for UBS (1992-2006) in both the Investment Banking and Wealth Management divisions. Initially based in London and later in Madrid, he was involved in M&A, IPO’s, equity derivative structures while at Corporate Finance. During his years in the Wealth Management side of the business, he launched a Corporate Finance capacity dedicated to private clients (where he worked with Javier Torremocha for 5 years) as well as the Key Clients Group, covering the largest private clients in Spain.

José María began his professional career in 1986 working at the consulting division of Arthur Andersen (now Accenture), gaining experience on information systems, programming and strategic management consulting. Later in 1988 he worked for Advent International, a private equity firm, generating deal flow, doing financial analysis and due diligence.

He holds a Law degree from Universidad Complutense in Madrid and an MBA from ESADE in Barcelona. He has also been designated as a Chartered Alternative Investment Analyst (CAIA).

Sonia Fernández

Partner

Sonia has over 16 years of experience leading digital businesses ranging from online auctions, digital media, online dating, advertising and ad technology. Sonia has also been an Angel investor and advisor to a number of companies such as ideas4all, hiphunters, clippingbook and smartycontent.

Sonia started her digital career at MercadoLibre in 1999, as part of the founding team and country manager of MercadoLibre in Spain. Sonia then moved on to the digital media sector at Prisacom (Grupo Prisa), Spain’s largest media group, where she helped shape elpais.com digital strategy. In 2003 Sonia launched Match.com in Spain, Italy and LatinAmerica and remained country manager in Spain until 2009. Sonia continued to develop her digital career in online advertising at Specific Media (US advertsing network) where she held the position of General Manager for Europe at VINDICO (video adserving) launching the service in the UK, France, Germany and Spain. Sonia has also executed consulting projects for FON (wifi) and smartycontent (video platform).

Sonia’s background is in investment banking where she worked in the Corporate Finance group of Lehman Brothers in London and New York and she has also experience in private equity where she was an associate at 3i in Spain.

Sonia holds a BA degree in Business from ICADE (Spain) and an MBA from Stanford University. Sonia has published two books on networking and social networks in 2004 and 2011, was awarded the Stanford Associate Award (limited to 2,700 alumni worldwide), and is currently Vice President of the Stanford Alumni Association in Spain. She is also member of the International Women Forum. Sonia speaks English, French and German. Sonia joined Lehman Brothers in London with Javier and has known Aquilino since 1999.

Pedro Torrecillas

Operating Partner

Teo Ruiz

Operating Partner

Teo has been involved with the tech scene for more than 15 years, managing engineering teams and tech strategy for top-tier internet companies in Spain and the UK. Teo is currently cofounder at Vernon, a consultancy firm helping growth-stage startups scale their product and engineering practices. He’s also an Expert in Residence at Google Campus Madrid.
