Advancing Women in Investing is a collaborative
network of more than 70 first-time and emerging 
female managers focused on frontier and emerging
markets who support each other to share learnings, co-
invest and raise capital. 
Our long-term goal is to create a movement of change 
to democratize access to funding and opportunity for 
emerging female capital movers. 
We want to accelerate access to funding and 
partnerships, affordable tools and resources, networking 
opportunities, deal sourcing and co-investors.
Through thought leadership, we will use what we learn 
from our network to change mindsets about investing 
in women investors.
Supporting women promotes inclusive growth.
Catalyzing innovative investments and supporting 
robust market growth starts with ensuring female capital 
movers are represented on investment teams and 
receive backing as managers.
Yet, a significant gap exists for women to access capital 
at all levels. We want to change this. 
In the following pages, you will find a cadre of forward-
looking female capital movers utilizing a broad spectrum 
of strategies to target high-quality private investment 
opportunities on the African continent. We encourage 
you to reach out if you would like more information on 
an individual manager.
