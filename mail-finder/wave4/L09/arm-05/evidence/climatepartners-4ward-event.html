4WARD Global Climate & Sustainability Networking Meetups DC & Baltimore

July 24, 2024 5:30 pm - 8:00 pm Franklin Hall, Washington DC

Join the Climate 4WARD DC Member Meetup to meet other climate professionals and like-minded individuals passionate about combatting climate change, using technology, and innovating to fight for Team Planet.

To register for this meet-up, visit the Eventbrite page. For more information, contact Justin Brodie-Kommit at Justin@JustinBk.com or Franz Hochstrasser at franz@aya.yale.edu to reach out with questions about the event, attendance, or any changes.
