ABOUT US

Unlocking Africa’s overlooked potential – starting in Francophone markets & scaling regionally

At Jambaar Capital, we’re more than a VC firm – we’re a catalyst for change across Francophone Africa and the broader North and West African regions. Led by a dynamic female team, we partner with visionary founders in vital sectors to drive inclusive, sustainable growth rooted in local insight and global ambition.

Our mission is clear: to empower visionary founders building impactful companies that shape the future of Francophone Africa, while remaining open to exceptional opportunities across North and West Africa. We invest with purpose – guided by principles of responsibility, gender balance, and sustainability – to drive growth that uplifts communities.

Based in the heart of Dakar, we sit at the crossroads of Francophone Africa’s emerging tech scene. With a front-row seat to the region’s rapid growth – and Africa’s broader 38% VC CAGR – we’re primed to lead in early-stage investing, combining local depth with strategic foresight.

Backed by three generations of entrepreneurial legacy and deep financial and legal expertise, we bring more than capital – we bring partnership. With a profound understanding of the regional ecosystem and trusted regional networks, we equip founders with the tools to scale and thrive.
