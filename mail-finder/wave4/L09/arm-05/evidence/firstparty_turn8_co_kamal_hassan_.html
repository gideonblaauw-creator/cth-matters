Kamal Hassan is the Founder & Managing Partner of TURN8 — the venture operating partner for GCC corporates and sovereign-linked institutions. Kamal has built a rare track record at the intersection of corporate innovation, venture capital, and operational execution across the United States, MENA, and Europe.

A 3× founder, he has co-built and exited multiple technology companies, and as a General Partner, has deployed $500M+ in capital across corporate venture capital platforms, VC funds, and syndicates. His work reflects a rare combination of founder instinct and investor discipline, with a portfolio that includes unicorn-scale outcomes and multiple Series A–D exits.

Kamal’s work is closely aligned with Saudi Vision 2030, where he partners with GCC national champions, government entities, and Global 1000 corporations. His focus includes designing and operating corporate venture capital programs, launching new ventures, building in-house AI Studios, and enabling international startups to scale into the Kingdom and the wider Gulf region.
