Investment thesis

Pachamama Ventures invests at the pre-seed stage in companies driving what it calls the Second Industrialization — the replacement of legacy physical infrastructure with breakthrough climate technologies. It focuses on three areas: advanced materials that make industrial processes cleaner and cheaper, energy systems for generation, storage and distribution that decarbonize at scale, and industrial platforms using AI, software and data to optimize the physical economy. The firm's differentiator is commercial access, drawing on its Managing Partner's 16 years of operating experience inside Fortune 100 companies to connect portfolio companies with enterprise procurement relationships. It positions the reindustrialization of the economy as both a major business opportunity and a climate imperative.
