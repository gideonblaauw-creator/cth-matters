“We invest in companies that uplift communities”

Do well by doing good

This is the founding principle that shapes Goodwell’s entire investment philosophy. Our funds support entrepreneurs delivering essential goods and services to un(der)served populations and building an inclusive economy in their communities, countries, and continent.

We maximise the social and financial impact of every investment we make by focussing on innovative, fast-growing, local companies.

Meet our portfolio companies

Goodwell invests in innovative companies providing essential goods and services in high-impact sectors like agriculture, financial services, and mobility and logistics sectors.

Goodwell's portfolio

Local investments, led by local experts

We believe that an inclusive society begins with a deep understanding of local markets, consumers and needs. All our investments are led by experts based in our focus regions, from our offices in Kenya, Nigeria and South Africa. Combined with operational support from our team in the Netherlands, we offer our portfolio companies unparalleled local insights, paired with access to experts and markets across the African continent and beyond.

First movers

A collaborative approach with meaningful returns

An inclusive society means no one is left behind. At Goodwell, we invest in innovative companies providing basic goods and services to those who need it most. By providing private equity to scalable companies in their early growth stage, we’re enabling them to maximise their impact. To date, our 20+ portfolio companies provided EUR 2.5 billion worth of financial services to more than 39 million households, created over 35,000 jobs, and reached communities across 68 countries.
