Acting together for greater impact

Our mission

Proparco provides funding and support to businesses and financial institutions in Africa, Asia, Latin America, Eurasia and the Middle-East. Subsidiary of the AFD Group, its interventions aim to strengthen the contribution of private players to the achievement of the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). Its action focuses on three impact objectives:

  • Sustainable and resilient economy
  • Protecting the planet
  • Reducing inequalities
