LAVCA Women

Women investors and founders remain underrepresented in Latin America, mirroring global trends. In private capital, women hold only ~10% of senior roles, and just 17% of venture-backed startups worldwide have a female co-founder. This portal consolidates LAVCA’s key directories, surveys, interviews and gender-diversity data across asset classes in the Latin American investment ecosystem.

Methodology

LAVCA’s Top and Emerging Women Investors in Latin America lists recognize women who are actively contributing to the region’s private capital and technology ecosystems through their work on investment teams and as angel investors. All women showcased must meet the following baseline criteria:

  • Active investment role: The candidate must be actively involved within the firm’s investment team, whether through sourcing, investment analysis and due diligence, or direct participation in investment-making decisions. 
  • Latin America investment focus: The candidate’s responsibilities and focus must explicitly include Latin America or one or more of its countries. For professionals at global investment firms, LAVCA requires confirmation (either directly from the firm or through secondary sources, such as the firm’s website or public materials) that Latin America is a defined part of the candidate’s mandate.

 

Top Women Investors in Latin America

The Top Women Investors in Latin America list recognizes senior-level female investment professionals and select angel investors meeting the following criteria:

  • Asset classes: Senior women on investment teams in private capital strategies, including Venture Capital, Growth Equity, Private Equity, Real Estate, Infrastructure, Natural Resources and Private Credit. 
  • Angel investors: Female angel investors who are investing in three or more Latin American tech startups per year, with at least USD50k per year deployed directly into startups in the region. 
  • Seniority and titles: Candidates who explicitly hold one of the following titles are eligible for consideration: Chairman, CEO, Senior Partner, Founding Partner, General Partner, Partner, or Head (e.g., Head of Investments, Head of Venture Capital, Head of Latin America). 
  • Track record and time frame: Investors must be active within these roles over the last five years.
  • Full-time status: Only investors with a full-time position within a firm are eligible for consideration, with the exception of active angel investors who may operate independently or alongside other professional roles.

 

Emerging Women Investors in Latin America

The Emerging Women Investors in Latin America list recognizes junior- and mid-level female investment professionals with responsibilities within investment teams. Eligible candidates must meet the following criteria:

  • Titles: Candidates must explicitly hold one of the following titles: Analyst, Associate, Senior Associate, Vice President, Principal, Investment Officer, or Director.
  • Active investment involvement: LAVCA must have confirmation that the candidate is actively involved in investment-making decisions within the firm. This can include responsibilities such as sourcing, evaluating and modeling potential investments, and/or participating in investment committees.

 

Confirmation Process

LAVCA relies on a combination of peer nominations, direct confirmations from firms and independent research by LAVCA Research to identify and evaluate candidates. All profiles are vetted to ensure alignment with the criteria above and to reflect the diversity of strategies, geographies and firm types that characterize Latin America’s evolving private capital ecosystem.
