Impact Investor Azzi Agnelli is backing Mental Health Ventures for Systemic Change

Role Model Leading By Example
Stephanie Ferrario
Jul 04, 2025

In today's episode of SheCan Media, Stephanie sits down with Azzi Agnelli to discuss investing in mental health for systemic change. Azzi is an impact investor, philanthropist, and dedicated mental health advocate whose work sits at the intersection of innovation, healing, and social reform. Azzi shares her deeply personal and professional mission: to elevate global mental health consciousness and fuel a future where care is holistic, inclusive, and truly accessible. As a General Partner at TMV: Lifecycles, she co-manages a groundbreaking fund focused on solving today’s mental health crisis by backing visionary entrepreneurs and practitioners building transformative solutions. With TMV’s latest initiative, MWBF, Azzi is helping bring bold new models of care to life—ones that combine data, technology, and empathy to empower both patients and providers. Her leadership is helping to reshape what mental health care looks like today—and what it could become tomorrow. In this conversation, you’ll learn why Azzi believes the mental health crisis requires urgent, systemic reform and how TMV is funding startups redefining care with innovation and empathy. The importance of treating the whole person, not just symptoms. How impact investing is uniquely positioned to accelerate mental wellness and finally Azzi’s academic and personal journey into psychology, purpose, and leadership.
