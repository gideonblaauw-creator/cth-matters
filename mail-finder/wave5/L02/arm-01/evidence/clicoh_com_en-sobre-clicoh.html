Our Story

The path we have traveled

2018

Delivery app

clicOH was founded in Argentina as a delivery app, taking the first steps in logistics.

2019

E-commerce logistics

We evolved into a last-mile logistics company specialized in e-commerce.

2020

Regional consolidation

We consolidated operations in Argentina and expanded into Uruguay, strengthening regional presence.

2021

Y Combinator + Mexico

We joined Y Combinator, the world's leading startup accelerator. We opened operations in Mexico.

2022

USD 33M Series A & Seed

We raised USD 33 million in Series A and Seed rounds to accelerate expansion in Latin America.

2023

Logysto acquisition

We acquired Logysto in Colombia, reinforcing our position in the Colombian market.

2025

50M+ processed packages

We consolidated operations in all active countries, surpassed 50 million packages, and added top Latin American brands.
