Chip Hazard

Co-founder & General Partner

INVESTMENT INTERESTS
AI Infrastructure • Developer Platforms • Agentic Business Applications

Chip's investment interests cover companies building data infrastructure and developer platforms for AI application builders and operators, and Agentic Business Applications. He is the longest serving non-founder board member at MongoDB and is also a co-founder and investment partner with XFactor Ventures, a Flybridge NextWave fund focused on investing in companies founded by women. Before co-founding the firm in 2002, Chip was a General Partner with Greylock, a leading venture capital firm he joined in 1994. Chip received a BA with honors from Stanford University and an MBA from Harvard Business School, where he was a Baker Scholar and a Ford Scholar.

Jeff Bussgang

Co-founder & General Partner

INVESTMENT INTERESTS
Agentic Business Applications • Vertical SaaS • Fintech

An entrepreneur, professor, and author, Jeff has lived the journey from inception to IPO. He serves on the Harvard Business School faculty where he teaches Launching Technology Ventures, a popular MBA class on entrepreneurship and AI. Prior to co-founding Flybridge, Jeff co-founded Upromise, a loyalty marketing and financial services firm that was acquired by Sallie Mae and was an executive team member at e-commerce pioneer Open Market (IPO 1996). Jeff's written three books, including his latest – The Experimentation Machine: Finding Product-Market Fit in the Age of AI.

Jesse Middleton

General Partner

INVESTMENT INTERESTS
Future of Work • Native AI Applications • Serial Entrepreneurs

Jesse spotted the remote work revolution over a decade before COVID. As an early WeWork executive who helped scale the company to 4,000 employees across multiple continents, and later as an angel investor with a 16x portfolio MOIC, Jesse has consistently backed transformative companies reshaping how we work, collaborate, and build community. In the last few years, Jesse's focus has turned to investing in Full-Stack AI Native Applications. Jesse became a General Partner at Flybridge in 2016 and has been an integral part of the last three funds leading investments in companies like Arcee, CarEdge, Chief, HiFi (acquired by Square), Imperfect Foods (acquired by Misfits Markets), Splice, and Teal.

Dorothy Chang

Venture Partner

Progress > INVESTMENT INTERESTS
automation • B2B & B2C • AI Software & Services

Dorothy Chang is a Venture Partner at Flybridge Capital Partners, investing in pre-seed and seed stage AI startups. She is also a Co-Founder of Lynx Collective, a weekly event series that connects early stage founders with critical people and resources in the NYC startup ecosystem. Previously she served as President of non-profit Kode With Klossy, working alongside Founder & supermodel Karlie Kloss to bring coding education to thousands of young women every year. Over her career, Dorothy has invested in startups as a founding partner of Liberty City Ventures (now with $1B+ AUM), a communications partner at Primary Venture Partners and an active angel investor in multiple unicorns.

Anna Palmer

Venture Partner

INVESTMENT INTERESTS
Agentic Business Applications • Marketplaces

Anna is a seasoned investor backing women and gender-diverse teams across the US, Europe and Latin America. She co-founded XFactor Ventures with Flybridge Capital in 2016—now one of the most active seed-stage funds backing women with over 100 investments. As Partner at Flybridge Capital, she has led investments in Chief, Zubale, MixLab, and Venus Aerospace. Anna is also founder and Managing Partner of Boston's National Women's Soccer League team, launching in 2026. Previously, she co-founded and led two successful companies: Dough Collective and Fashion Project, both acquired after distributing funds to over 2,000 organizations globally. Anna holds a BA from Eureka College and a JD from Harvard Law School.

Daniel Porras Reyes

Associate

INVESTMENT INTERESTS
AI Enablement • Developer Platforms • Memory & Inference

Daniel's investment interests include companies that are developing data infrastructure and platforms for AI application builders and operators. Prior to joining Flybridge, Daniel worked as an Associate at Dalus Capital, where he led the opening of their Colombian office. Before Dalus, he was a Manager at Velum Ventures. Daniel began his professional career as an entrepreneur, founding a fintech savings company called "Monedero." This company was selected to participate in an acceleration program by Google and was recognized as one of the top 10 most promising fintech startups in the country in 2018.

Cheraé Robinson

Head of Platform and Marketing

INVESTMENT INTERESTS
Consumer AI • Deep Tech • Underrepresented Founders

Cheraé Robinson is an entrepreneur and cultural architect leading portfolio support, brand, and community at Flybridge. She makes pre-seed investments through Next Wave NYC, focusing on AI and the future of place and belonging, backing companies like Civic Reach, Falcon, BiteSight, and Babel Loft. Understanding first-time founder challenges from her own experience building Tastemakers Africa (with Flybridge as a seed investor), she hosts the First-Time Founders Food Club, a monthly dinner series. She is the co-host of "It Just Got Real" podcast with Hannah Donovan and has been featured in Forbes, TED, TechCrunch, and Condé Nast Traveler. She holds a B.S. in Biology from Morgan State University and prior to entering startupland held various roles in international organizations like CARE, the CDC, and CIMMYT.

Matt Guiney

CFO

Finance • Operations • Compliance

Matt leads the firm's financial, operational, and administrative activities including accounting, reporting, tax, legal, fund formation, compliance, regulatory, and information systems. Prior to joining Flybridge in May 2009, Matt spent six years at PricewaterhouseCoopers where he specialized in venture capital and private equity auditing. Before PwC, Matt spent two years at Babson Capital serving in various accounting and finance roles. Matt graduated cum laude from Northeastern University and is a Certified Public Accountant.

Scott
