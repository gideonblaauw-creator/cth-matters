Eduardo Campos

Partner

Eduardo Campos is a Co-founder and Partner at ONEVC.

Eduardo is a former management consultant (Monitor Group) and has been an active member of the local tech ecosystem as both an operator and investor. Since 2012, Eduardo has backed companies such as Gympass, Creditas, and QuintoAndar. Additionally, Eduardo has founded two companies: Parafuzo (’14), one of the first end-to-end local services marketplaces in Brazil and Yuca (’19), a leading operator of residential real estate. Eduardo is passionate about supporting other entrepreneurs, especially in the earliest days of a company’s journey, across software, marketplace, fintech, and internet/consumer business.

‍

City:
São Paulo, Brazil
Graduation:

B.A. in Music, Berklee College

Extensions Courses, Harvard Business School
