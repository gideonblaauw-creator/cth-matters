    GREEN BOND CORPORATION GROUP (GBC GROUP)?

    Combining deep expertise and global thought leadership in sustainable finance, infrastructure development and carbon-based financing, GBC Group and its highly renowned team are considered thought leaders, who collectively together provide a unique, yet highly refined abilities. Whether located locally, nationally, regionally or globally, suitably qualified projects can benefit from various components of the capital financing stack, including equity, development financing, project financing, bond issuances as well as carbon-based financing. Climate change response, sustainability, social and humanitarian agendas have increasingly become global priorities and consequently, the next ten years must truly become the ‘decadeof- action’ addressing such critically important issues.
