Our Team

Michael Acampora

Advisor, Strategic Projects & Partnerships

Michael Acampora is the Advisor, Strategic Projects & Partnerships at Pegasus Capital Advisors focused on special projects, external partnerships, and marketing. Prior to joining Pegasus, Michael spent nearly 15 years working on public policy, project management, and research in the police reform and violence prevention spaces. He led individual and systemic police misconduct investigations at the New York City Civilian Complaint Review Board and the Office of the Inspector General for the NYPD, and was a Research and Policy Advisor at John Jay College of Criminal Justice’s National Network for Safe Communities, an action research center focused on evidence-based violence reduction strategies. Michael graduated from Brown University with a BA in Political Science and Literary Arts.

Wataru Baba

Senior Strategic Advisor

Wataru Baba is a Senior Strategic Advisor at Pegasus Capital and a distinguished leader in innovation and sustainability with over 20 years of experience across multiple sectors. He was the former Group CTO of Climate, Energy, and Environment at Panasonic Group and head of Sustainability and Climate business, including North America’s largest EV battery. In 2019, he became Panasonic’s youngest ever Corporate Executive Officer. He led the formulation of the Panasonic GREEN IMPACT, aiming to reduce global CO2 emissions by 1% through product innovations.

Mr. Baba’s pioneering work in climate impact management with Avoided Emissions drove large-scale behavioral change, resulting in the publication of guidelines on WBCSD’s Avoided Emissions and recognition in the G7 Leaders’ Communiqué to accelerate societal net-zero goals. He has demonstrated how effective impact management can revolutionize corporate climate actions, achieving positive outcomes for both people and the planet while driving significant business growth. His work has been recognized by Mission Innovation, a global initiative to accelerate clean energy innovation. He is now applying his expertise to non-energy emission sectors, addressing issues such as methane and nitrous oxide emissions from livestock, innovating crops and grain production to avoid deforestation, and utilizing precision fermentation, cultivated meat, air protein, and seaweed.

Previously, he held senior management positions at SAP in Tokyo, Newtown Square, and Palo Alto. Currently, he advises climate and nature companies listed on CA100+ and NA100 across energy, food and agriculture, forest, and financial institutions. He also serves as a board member of the Japan Professional Football League (J-League). Japan’s Weekly Gendai magazine named him one of the “50 CEOs Who Will Change Japan’s Future” among the 100 top influencers from business, science, and the arts. He is married and a father of two daughters. He lives with two cats in California.

Craig Cogut

Founder, Chairman & CEO

Craig Cogut has spent a career building successful investment businesses. In 1990, Craig co-founded Apollo Advisors L.P. (now Apollo Global Management), and in 1996, Craig founded Pegasus Capital Advisors, a private equity fund. Craig currently serves as Pegasus’ Chairman and CEO, and is a member of the management and investment committees. Through Craig’s leadership, Pegasus has evolved to meet the most pressing challenges of our time: climate resilience, food and water security, and sustainable economic development. This mission led to Pegasus becoming the first U.S. private equity fund manager to be accredited by the Green Climate Fund. Craig now leads Pegasus’ ongoing partnerships with international organizations such as the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) and the United Nations Joint SDG Fund, and he is one of the Founding Co-Chairs of the SDG Digital Transformation and Sustainability Solutions Lab, a Pegasus-sponsored initiative that works with the UNDP to support collaborations to advance the 2030 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). In addition, Craig has been an active philanthropist in the fields of improving education, building civil society, and championing environmental and health issues. He currently serves as Chairman of the finance advisory board of the Global Alliance for a Sustainable Planet, a non-profit focused on leveraging private finance for public good in order to achieve sustainable development goals. Craig is an alumnus of Brown University and Harvard Law School.
