Our Approach

  • Partnering with proven* tech & tech-enabled businesses, helping them scale and become world-class organizations
  • Targeting Scalability, Profitable Growth, Culture, and Teamwork

Our Focus
We invest in
Technology and technology-related businesses, with large market opportunities and demonstrated business models, which can accelerate growth if provided the necessary capital and support. We seek these opportunities on a global basis, with particular emphasis on North America, Latin America, and other select emerging markets.
Investment sizes
We typically evaluate and lead investments that range from $25 – $250+ million per company. Riverwood’s investment strategy is predicated on how we assess the long-term potential of a business, and whether we can help with our Scalability Playbook. We routinely help high-growth companies navigate strategic or operational points of inflection.
