OKAVANGO CAPITAL PARTNERS

Investment Value Through Natural Capital
Okavango Capital Partners is a commercial investment firm focused on reducing climate risks and biodiversity loss in Africa's important ecosystems by working closely with and promoting the well being of local communities. Okavango harnesses the power of markets to promote sustainable behaviours at scale to prevent key drivers of nature's degradation.
​
Africa's remaining natural ecosystems are facing unprecedented pressure from demographic and market-based forces: fast population growth, increasing inequality between rural and urban populations, and rising vulnerability to climate change.
​​
Markets have a key role to play in shaping how Africa's rapidly growing population will consume food, materials, energy and leisure. Businesses and investors have the power to cause significant damage, but also significant good to Africa’s ecosystems.
