 TEAM

Josep Oriol
​Managing Partner
​Josep has extensive experience in investments, law and private equity spanning over 20 years in Africa, Europe, USA and Japan: first in Conservation Capital and subsequently at Kibo capital partners an SME private equity investor with $100M under management. Josep has acquired substantial experience in environmental conservation through investments in wildlife, tourism, forestry, carbon and his role on several board positions. He holds an MBA from Duke University, a degree in Law  (LLB) from Universidad Internacional  de Catalunya and a Bachelor of Arts in Political Science from Universidad Autonoma de Barcelona.

Caroline Chepkwony
​Partner
​​​Caroline is a seasoned finance professional with over 25 years of experience in private equity and  finance in Africa.  She has extensive experience in Fund management, mobilizing capital and structuring transactions. She is the founder of Pesa Crowd an investment platform focused on mobilising capital for high-impact opportunities.
She previously served as Chief Finance Officer and Head of Investor Relations at Fanisi Capital, where she played a key role in fund management, financial strategy, and managing relationships with international development finance institutions and institutional investors. Earlier in her career, Caroline worked at PricewaterhouseCoopers, where she held roles in finance and business advisory, supporting corporate clients on financial management, governance and transaction-related matters. She holds an MBA from the University of Oxford.

Davinder Sikand 
Senior Advisor
Davinder is one of the private equity pioneer with over 30 years' experience in Africa,  Europe and USA. He co-founded Aureos a mid market  private equity firm where served as Deputy CEO and Head of Sub- Saharan African Operations. He later initiated the sale of Aureos to Abraaj group, a US$14 Bn emerging market asset manager where he headed African Operations. Davinder has extensive experience in Environmental Conservation through board positions. He holds an MBA from Kellogg.  ​​​​​

Giles Davies
​Senior Advisor
Giles has over 20 years’ experience in private equity and impact investing in Africa, Europe and USA. He founded Conservation Capital (CC) and is widely recognized as one the of world’s leading specialists in conservation enterprise development and related investment financing.  He started his career at Price Waterhouse Coopers. In addition to his work with CC, Giles has considerable experience as a non-executive director of both private and listed companies.​

Judith Minoo
​​Analyst
Judith is a finance and investment professional with a varsity of experience working in Venture Capital, Investment management offering financial advisory, Valuation and fundraising services for MSMEs & SME markets in East Africa.  Prior to joining Okavango, she worked as an Investment Analyst at Cornerstone Enterprises Plc and Raisin Capital. She holds a Bachelor of Science in Actuarial Science from Karatina University. She’s a certified Financial Modelling and Valuation Analyst by Corporate Finance Institute.​​

Caroline Njoki
​Operations & Impact Analyst
​With a wealth of 8+ years of professional expertise spanning Office Administration, Customer Experience, Caroline brings a multifaceted skill set to her role. Her specialization lies in Impact measurement and management, Executive support and streamlined Business Operations. Before becoming an integral part of Okavango, Caroline worked at Samsung Electronics. She has a  Bachelor of Arts Degree in Public Relations from the University of Nairobi.

Nyokabi Manguyu
Investment Committee

Nyokabi was previously at Goldman Sachs, and Merryl Lynch. She is a CFA charter holder and has a BSc in Economics and Finance by Ithaca College, NY​.

Jon Mellberg
Investment Committee

He spent ten years as a Managing Director at Tudor, Pickering, Holt & Co., a leading energy investment and merchant bank. In addition to his management experience, he's held roles in institutional sales, capital markets  and research.  He serves as a board chairman at Conservation Strategy Fund. ​

Charlotte Ward
Investment Committee
​​Charlotte is a Managing director at the Global Innovation Fund. Previously she was a senior partner at AHL Venture Partners, the manager of impact investment for the Lundin family office and the AHL Growth Fund.  Charlotte spent the last ten years in Kenya working in development and investment (grant and venture capital) in early stage businesses.  Prior to that she worked at GSMA and Deutsche Bank where she was vice president. Charlotte serves on several SMEs boards and holds a Masters in Applied Environment Studies from the University of Bristol​.

Elizabeth Littlefield
​Advisor
​Elizabeth is a Senior Counselor at Albright Stonebridge Group, a global business strategy firm. Previously, she was the President and CEO of OPIC with a $24 billion portfolio to support private investment in sustainable economic development. From 2000 until 2010 Littlefield was a director at the World Bank and the CEO of CGAP, a policy and research center for inclusive finance. Earlier in her career Littlefield was a Managing Director at JP Morgan’s capital markets and financing for Europe, Middle East and Africa. Elizabeth serves on several boards: The World Wildlife Fund, DAI, and Mkopa. She has also served on boards of Master card Foundation and Calvert Foundation.

Christine Hemrick
Advisor

Advisor

Andrea Athanas
Advisor
