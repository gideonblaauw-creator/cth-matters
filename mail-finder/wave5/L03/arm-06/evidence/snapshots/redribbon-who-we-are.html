The Firm

Established in 2007, Red Ribbon is a leading impact investment company based in the UK. We offer our clients the opportunity to participate in a diversified portfolio of projects, all of which have the potential to become profitable unicorns.

Red Ribbon has operations in the United Kingdom, Isle of Man, Gibraltar, The Netherlands, Germany, Mauritius and India. Owned by founding directors and shareholders, our close-knit team has worked together since inception. We are inspired by a shared commitment to influencing the future of the global economy in a responsible, sustainable direction.

Our Team

Red Ribbon is led by Suchit Punnose, the CEO and Founder. Punnose, an entrepreneur and investor, has extensive expertise in investment and operations in the UK and in growth markets. The team focuses on developing pioneering investment strategies through single companies and regulated funds offering sectoral focus and diversification based on investor preferences.

The management team has a deep knowledge base across several sectors. This base is founded on years of experience applying the principles of impact investment to the formulation of net zero solutions. Core sectors of expertise include property, hospitality, infrastructure and renewable energy in growth markets. We model the change we seek by operating as a net zero enterprise.
