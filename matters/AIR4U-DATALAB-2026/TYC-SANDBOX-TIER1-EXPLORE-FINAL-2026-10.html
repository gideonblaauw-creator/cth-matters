1. Parties and roles

The Innovation Partner brings the technology, collects the raw sensor data, and runs one or more sandboxes per vertical. The Implementation Partner (for example a clinic) receives the output in a private sandbox, isolated from the others. The Custodian designs the measurement, cleans and presents it, and does not collect the raw data. The Custodian bills every tier, including Diagnose and Verify.

1. Partes y roles

El Socio Innovador aporta la tecnología, recoge el dato crudo del sensor y administra uno o varios sandboxes por vertical. El Socio Implementador (por ejemplo, una clínica) recibe la salida en un sandbox privado, aislado de los demás. El Custodio diseña la medición, la limpia y la presenta, y no recoge el crudo. El Custodio factura todos los tiers, incluidos Diagnose y Verify.

2. Tier 1, and what sits outside it

Tier 1 “Explore” is free forever. The output is a snapshot of indoor air in a single area and its baseline against outdoor air. It includes no verification, report or certificate. Diagnose (USD 4,000 per site report), Verify (from USD 7,500 per statement, due when issued, whether pass or fail), and the end-of-pilot report that triggers payment are governed by their own contracts. The Custodian bills those contracts. The price the Innovation Partner charges the Implementation Partner, with or without the Innovation Partner’s own margin, does not include the Custodian as a party.

2. Tier 1, y lo que queda fuera

El Tier 1 «Explore» es gratuito para siempre. La salida es un corte del aire interior de una sola área y su línea de base frente al aire exterior. No incluye verificación, informe ni certificado. Diagnose (USD 4.000 por informe de sitio), Verify (desde USD 7.500 por declaración, que se causa al emitirla, con pass o con fail) y el informe de cierre de piloto que causa el pago se rigen por sus propios contratos. Esos contratos los factura el Custodio. El precio que el Socio Innovador cobre al Socio Implementador, con margen propio o sin él, no tiene al Custodio como parte.

This clause is the transmission contract under Decreto 1377 de 2013, art. 25. The Implementation Partner holds the authorisation required by Ley 1581, or a statutory exception, before personal data are loaded. The Innovation Partner does not load personal data without that authorisation. In a health setting, a series that reveals a person’s health is sensitive data (arts. 5 and 6) and is not processed without explicit authorisation or a statutory exception.
