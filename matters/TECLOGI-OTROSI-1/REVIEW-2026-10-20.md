## 2. Top 5 issues

**1. The first US$500,000 of a larger cheque is free, and Innovbo says that is how the original equity schedule already works.** Gideon's 30 Sep example charges the higher rate on the first full million (US$5M mezzanine = US$45,000). Clause 3.6 and the rewritten examples charge it only on the slice between US$500,000 and US$1,000,000, put everything above US$20,000,000 at zero, and say this "precisa" the original Anexo 1. On the US$6M equity cheque in section 4, origination falls from US$70,000 to US$60,000. Clause 8 then says the otrosí wins any conflict with the original contract, so the cut is not confined to hybrid and debt. The printed 2.0% / 1.0% rates do not change. The dollars do.

**2. Hybrid and debt are taken out of the Round, and past work can be forfeited on a 10-business-day notice.** The 30 Sep draft rewrote the Round so hybrid and debt sat inside it. Innovbo keeps the Round as equity, says these instruments do not count toward the US$13M target or final closing, and requires the cash by twelve months after 1 August 2026. Clause 6C.1 then completes the original termination clause so the contract can end when the equity round closes. Clause 7 tries to save fees that accrue "durante el período de tail", but that tail is not in this file **[VERIFICAR]**. Clause 6A.7 adds a separate trap: hybrid or debt counterparties CTH has been working since 1 August 2026 must be notified within 10 business days after this otrosí is signed, or there is no fee. Jop's third point was a long enough protection if closing drags. This markup does not do that.

**3.
