## Reviewer feedback (must address)

From GRP Challenge Fund, 14 Jul 2026:

> Clarify the **emergency context framing** more explicitly: while the post-conflict PDET setting is relevant, the proposal should articulate how the anticipatory action framework responds to **specific climate emergency events** rather than primarily to structural deforestation pressure. Alternatively, the **TECH4Nature** alignment is also strong and could be emphasised alongside the emergency framing.

Working choice for the full proposal: keep both windows, but make TECH4Emergencies rest on named climate triggers (IDEAM precipitation anomalies, drought/flood that collapse harvest income and force clearing), and make TECH4Nature rest on DIST-ALERT / Sentinel-2 plus 5 km buffer verification. Do not let “post-conflict” substitute for the emergency trigger.
