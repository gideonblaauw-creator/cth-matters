## 3. Project summary

Climate shocks and EUDR exclusion threaten women-led cacao and coffee cooperatives in Colombia’s PDET territories. When harvest income collapses, producers are pushed to clear forest. PDET-CIDM fuses Sentinel-2 and DIST-ALERT with CleantechHUB’s PDET warehouse to score climate-emergency and deforestation pressure, then triggers a 14-day bundle: community-validated geolocation, adaptation support, and buyer matchmaking. CleantechHUB and PASO Colombia will pilot with live cooperatives in Arauca, Guaviare, Istmina, Florida, and Fonseca. Expected results: 400 people supported (≥50% women or youth), USD 75,000 investments mobilised, EUDR dossiers, and standing-forest verification.

## 4. Problem statement

Colombia’s 170 PDET municipios remain climate and market frontlines. The gap is not missing satellite alerts or missing field programmes. The gap is the missing link between a **named climate emergency** that collapses harvest income and a **timed, community-owned response** that keeps legal agroforestry standing and saleable.

### Evidence (PASO August 2026 map)

- Operational geography is PASO’s live inventory, **not** the May concept-note list (Pacífico Nariñense / Putumayo).
- Global Forest Watch, 2002–2025, primary-forest loss in municipios PASO can reach:
  - San José del Guaviare: 140 kha
  - Arauquita: 14 kha (29% of remaining primary forest lost)
  - Tame: 7.1 kha
  - Istmina: 1.1 kha
  - Florida: 69 ha
  - Fonseca: 54 ha
- EUDR: December 2026 (large operators) and June 2027 (smallholders).
- Most PDET smallholders still lack plot-level, deforestation-free evidence since December 2020.

### Resilience framing

- **Of what:** household livelihoods in legal cacao, coffee and Amazonian products; cooperative governance; standing forest in 5 km buffers.
- **To what:** drought, flood, harvest failure, and IDEAM precipitation anomalies that cut income and precede clearing. Secondary: EUDR exclusion and access constraints.
- **For whom:** at least 400 women- and youth-majority producers. ≥50% women or youth.

## 5. Solution statement

### Elevator pitch

PDET-CIDM is an anticipatory-action layer that turns public satellite and climate signals into a 14-day, community-validated response for PASO-linked cooperatives.

### What already exists

- CleantechHUB PDET warehouse: 108,671 records, 170 municipios, live API at pdet-api.cleantechhub.net
- Climate Innovation Data Matching
- PASO field presence, some polygons, and security protocols

### What this grant buys

- Cooperative-level scoring layer on the warehouse
- 12-month four-phase pilot
- PASO USD 20,000 field package
- One GRP Induction Workshop trip to Nairobi
- Evidence GRP will audit

### Inputs to the score

- Copernicus Sentinel-2 (10 m, 5-day revisit)
- NASA/UMD DIST-ALERT
- IDEAM precipitation anomalies
- EUDR exposure
- Women / youth leadership

### 14-day bundle when a trigger fires

- EUDR-ready geolocation from **existing polygons and participatory mapping** — not external GPS brigades
- Climate-adaptation support through PASO’s Escuelas Rurales Alternativas
- Deforestation-free buyer matchmaking
- Climate-finance matching

### Why this beats the status quo

- Global Forest Watch alerts have no field owner
- FNC SICA geolocates coffee after the fact
- Humanitarian anticipatory action targets weather extremes, not commodity-driven clearing
- Nobody in these municipios joins alert + warehouse + 14-day PASO response + EUDR dossier + buyer

### Method locked with PASO (August)

1. Satellite and existing records first
2. Participatory maps in cooperative seats and municipal centres
3. Local verification only when EUDR requires it, after a formal security assessment, by people the community already knows
4. **No external GPS brigades**
5. **No contact with armed groups**

## 6. Thematic challenge alignment

Do not paste the May concept note here.

### Primary — TECH4Nature

- AI and remote sensing protect and manage nature around live agroforestry organisations
- DIST-ALERT and Sentinel-2 detect emerging degradation
- Communities validate polygons without outside GPS brigades
- Repeat passes over 5 km buffers verify standing forest
- EUDR dossiers make regulation a reason to keep forest, not abandon legal crops

### Secondary — TECH4Emergencies

Reviewer feedback (14 July 2026) required a **specific climate emergency**, not structural deforestation alone.

**Trigger pathway**

1. Named climate event (IDEAM anomaly, drought, flood, or harvest failure)
2. Income collapse
3. Clearing risk
4. DIST-ALERT confirms pressure
5. 14-day bundle

Post-conflict PDET status explains vulnerability. It is **not** the emergency.

### AI specs

- Four score classes: climate emergency, deforestation alert, EUDR exposure, women/youth leadership
- Monthly CTH–PASO case review
- PASO activates local liaisons only for prioritised organisations

## 10. Scaling pathway and additionality

- **Scaled model:** same scoring layer across the 16 PDET subregions; PASO or a peer owns the 14-day bundle
- **Demand:** EUDR dates + climate volatility
- **Evidence of use:** PASO Arauca cacao (BID Lab) sold 4,164 kg above market and raised women-led cacao incomes 149%
- **Pathway:** open methodology (EN/ES); ART conversations; buyer files reused in Alianzas Comerciales Colaborativas
- **Additionality:** GRP pays for the anticipatory layer and the PASO **cash** package. Warehouse and PASO in-kind stay outside the GRP sheet
- **2028–2029 (indicative):** 2,000 people; USD 400,000 mobilised; one extra PDET subregion
